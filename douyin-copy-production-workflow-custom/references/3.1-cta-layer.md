# 3.1 Gemini Full Retention And Fixed Ending

Read this file after a 3.1 Gemini body passes topic, count, force, and length checks and before title/opening packaging or Word export.

## Purpose

The accepted 3.1 body keeps every content unit returned by Gemini. Do not remove or rebuild its Doubao/AI-summary guidance, engagement prompts, fan-group language, account wording, examples, CTA units, hard-edged judgments, or repeated conversion mentions. Removing a redundant empty intensifying adverb is allowed only as a recorded narrow diction repair when the claim, force, cadence, syntax, and argument stay unchanged.

The exact opening-guidance unit must already be present in the Gemini raw output and must end with `好，我们直接进入正题。` Do not add or repair this unit during retention post-processing; reject and rerun the unchanged isolated request when it is missing, duplicated, or paraphrased.

The only mandatory content replacement is the terminal ending. The final sentence must be exactly:

`我是探花Gary，我们粉丝群里见，感谢观看。`

## Default Transformation

1. Save Gemini's raw response unchanged and record its SHA256.
2. Use the raw response as the accepted language surface. Preserve every paragraph and content-bearing sentence in the original order.
3. Do not run the legacy 3.1 conversion cleaner or zero-CTA validator. Do not add a separate Codex mid CTA or restore another opening-guidance unit.
4. Replace only Gemini's terminal ending with `我是探花Gary，我们粉丝群里见，感谢观看。` If Gemini already ends with that exact sentence, leave it unchanged.
5. Narrow grammar, punctuation, obvious broken-word, TTS, and redundant-adverb repairs are allowed only when they do not remove a content claim, example, conversion unit, force-bearing judgment, or change the argument. Record every repair as an exact nonempty `before`/`after` pair.
6. For redundant adverbs, review `极其 / 非常 / 十分 / 相当 / 无比 / 格外 / 极度` in context. Remove only occurrences that add no new degree contrast, consequence, or rhythm. Each tracked term may remain no more than `max(2, ceil(CJK/2500))` times, and their combined count may remain no more than `max(5, ceil(CJK/800))` times. These ceilings do not authorize automatic deletion: keep a necessary force-bearing occurrence, but then reduce other empty repetitions so the final still passes.
7. Never summarize, shorten, sanitize, calm, professionalize, reorganize, or delete Gemini content units merely because they sound repetitive, promotional, aggressive, theatrical, manipulative, or template-like.
8. Do not send the retained body back to Gemini for a second rewrite. Redundant-adverb cleanup is a local acceptance repair, not a regeneration trigger.

## Word Highlight Output

- Default 3.1 Word: highlight only the normalized fixed ending in yellow.
- Never highlight source text, Gemini body text, headings, title packaging, opening candidates, or mechanical repairs.
- If the user explicitly asks for an unhighlighted production copy, preserve the highlighted default and export the clean copy separately with `--allow-no-yellow`.
- Preserve two separate artifacts: immutable Gemini raw and final full-retention body.

Manifest shape:

```json
{
  "mode": "3.1_gemini_full_retention",
  "gemini_raw_path": "<immutable Gemini raw path>",
  "gemini_raw_sha256": "<sha256>",
  "gemini_content_preserved": true,
  "removed_content": [],
  "mechanical_repairs": [
    {"repair_type": "redundant_adverb_removal", "before": "<exact original text>", "after": "<same text with only an empty intensifier removed>"}
  ],
  "terminal_ending_before": "<exact terminal ending returned by Gemini>",
  "fixed_ending": "我是探花Gary，我们粉丝群里见，感谢观看。",
  "highlight_texts": ["我是探花Gary，我们粉丝群里见，感谢观看。"]
}
```

Use an empty `mechanical_repairs` list when no repair is needed. `terminal_ending_before` may equal `fixed_ending`.

## Acceptance Gate

Before export, verify:

- `gemini_raw_preserved: true`;
- `gemini_content_preserved: true`;
- `removed_content: []`;
- every mechanical repair has nonempty exact before/after text and is applied once in order;
- every tracked-adverb deletion is labelled `repair_type: redundant_adverb_removal`, removes only tracked empty intensifiers, and leaves all other text unchanged;
- each tracked term and their combined count stay within the formal 3.1 density ceilings;
- the fixed opening guidance appears exactly once and ends with `好，我们直接进入正题。`;
- no Codex CTA or conversion sentence was inserted;
- the final text equals the raw text after the recorded mechanical repairs and terminal-ending replacement;
- the exact fixed ending occurs once and is the final sentence;
- the fixed ending is the only yellow-highlighted text by default;
- the raw and final artifacts do not overwrite each other.

Reject and revise when any Gemini content unit disappears, paragraphs are reordered, a force-bearing word is removed, an unrecorded wording change appears, redundant tracked adverbs remain above the ceilings, a Codex CTA is added, the ending is not exact, or yellow highlighting includes anything besides the fixed ending.
