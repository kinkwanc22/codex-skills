# 3.1 Gemini Full Retention And Fixed Ending

Read this file after a 3.1 Gemini body passes topic, count, force, and length checks and before title/opening packaging or Word export.

## Purpose

The accepted 3.1 body keeps all content returned by Gemini. Do not remove or rebuild its Doubao/AI-summary guidance, engagement prompts, fan-group language, account wording, examples, CTA units, hard-edged language, or repeated conversion mentions.

The only mandatory content replacement is the terminal ending. The final sentence must be exactly:

`我是探花Gary，我们粉丝群里见，感谢观看。`

## Default Transformation

1. Save Gemini's raw response unchanged and record its SHA256.
2. Use the raw response as the accepted language surface. Preserve every paragraph and content-bearing sentence in the original order.
3. Do not run the legacy 3.1 conversion cleaner or zero-CTA validator. Do not add a separate Codex mid CTA or restore another opening-guidance unit.
4. Replace only Gemini's terminal ending with `我是探花Gary，我们粉丝群里见，感谢观看。` If Gemini already ends with that exact sentence, leave it unchanged.
5. Narrow grammar, punctuation, obvious broken-word, and TTS repairs are allowed only when they do not remove a content claim, example, conversion unit, or change the argument. Record every repair as an exact nonempty `before`/`after` pair.
6. Never summarize, shorten, sanitize, calm, professionalize, reorganize, or delete Gemini content merely because it sounds repetitive, promotional, aggressive, theatrical, manipulative, or template-like.
7. Do not send the retained body back to Gemini for a second rewrite.

## Word Highlight Output

- Default 3.1 Word: highlight only the normalized fixed ending in yellow.
- Never highlight source text, Gemini body text, headings, title packaging, opening candidates, or mechanical repairs.
- If the user explicitly asks for an unhighlighted production copy, preserve the highlighted default and export the clean copy separately with `--allow-no-yellow`.
- Preserve two separate artifacts: immutable Gemini raw and final full-retention body.

Manifest shape:

```json
{
  "mode": "3.1_gemini_full_retention",
  "gemini_raw_path": "<immutable Gemini raw path>",
  "gemini_raw_sha256": "<sha256>",
  "gemini_content_preserved": true,
  "removed_content": [],
  "mechanical_repairs": [
    {"before": "<exact original text>", "after": "<exact repaired text>"}
  ],
  "terminal_ending_before": "<exact terminal ending returned by Gemini>",
  "fixed_ending": "我是探花Gary，我们粉丝群里见，感谢观看。",
  "highlight_texts": ["我是探花Gary，我们粉丝群里见，感谢观看。"]
}
```

Use an empty `mechanical_repairs` list when no repair is needed. `terminal_ending_before` may equal `fixed_ending`.

## Acceptance Gate

Before export, verify:

- `gemini_raw_preserved: true`;
- `gemini_content_preserved: true`;
- `removed_content: []`;
- every mechanical repair has nonempty exact before/after text and is applied once in order;
- no Codex CTA or conversion sentence was inserted;
- the final text equals the raw text after the recorded mechanical repairs and terminal-ending replacement;
- the exact fixed ending occurs once and is the final sentence;
- the fixed ending is the only yellow-highlighted text by default;
- the raw and final artifacts do not overwrite each other.

Reject and revise when any Gemini content unit disappears, paragraphs are reordered, an unrecorded wording change appears, a Codex CTA is added, the ending is not exact, or yellow highlighting includes anything besides the fixed ending.
