# 2.5 Finished-Draft Transplant / 2.5成稿换芯

Use this mode only when the user supplies an already-expanded, user-approved old 2.5 manuscript and asks to keep its feel while replacing the internal content. It is distinct from routine `2.5 Direct Draft`.

## Current Canonical Mode

As of 2026-08-01, `2.5成稿换芯` always means the fused assembly validated by the user:

`complete July 5 original 2.5 block -> strong-attack transplant supplement -> article-specific new skeleton -> full approved old-2.5 finished draft`

This fused assembly supersedes the earlier layer-only transplant prompt. Never call the strong-attack transplant persona or task layer without first loading the complete original 2.5 block. The original block controls Gary voice, opening formula, spoken rhythm, case texture, mechanism density, and continuous deduction; the supplement controls mother-topic preservation, new-body construction, exclusion of copied old material, and attack-level retention.

## Trigger Phrases

- `2.5成稿换芯`
- `用已经扩写好的文案改内容`
- `保留母题，替换内容`
- `用刚才保留的模式`
- `按十二个黑暗思维/基因吸引那两篇的方法`

## Fixed Outcome

Preserve:

- the mother topic and its audience promise;
- the approved old 2.5 pressure, Gary voice, rough spoken rhythm, rhetorical questions, analogies, uneven pacing, and abrupt hard conclusions;
- distinctive source mechanisms only when the user explicitly says they must remain.

Replace:

- the old list items or internal argument chain;
- signature examples, dialogue, set pieces, and proof scenes;
- old metaphors or phrases that would make the new draft feel like a paraphrase.

The correct result is `same mother topic + same expressive DNA + genuinely new body`, not a renamed old draft and not a safe rewrite.

## Required Preparation

1. Extract the body of the supplied `.docx`, normally beginning after the `正文` heading.
2. Build two separate notes:
   - `style genes`: opening pressure, address terms, sentence rhythm, analogy type, list pacing, transition style, CTA placement, and ending cadence;
   - `content genes`: every major thesis, promised item, mechanism, signature case, dialogue, analogy, and conclusion.
3. Convert all old content genes into a concrete exclusion list. Name recognizable scenes and phrases instead of using a vague instruction such as `不要重复原文`.
4. Design a new internal skeleton before calling Gemini. Use ordinary spoken judgments, not abstract course-module labels.
5. Keep the mother topic fixed. A promised number may change only when the user permits it or the mother topic has no fixed-number promise. Every promised item must be delivered.

## Prompt Assembly

Use a fresh Mac Gemini `--isolated` request for every manuscript. Do not use the saved 2.5 conversation and do not send `/new`.

Assemble the prompt in this order:

1. the complete, verbatim 2026-07-05 original `2.5 Direct Draft` block, including its short `旧2.5风格参照` injection;
2. `【追加任务层：旧2.5成稿换芯】` and an explicit instruction that this layer supplements rather than replaces the original 2.5 block;
3. the fixed mother topic, full old-content exclusion list, and new content skeleton;
4. the strong-attack extension rules and hard output rules;
5. `【旧2.5成稿表达母版开始】...【旧2.5成稿表达母版结束】` containing the supplied finished draft body.

Never run the transplant persona/layer by itself. The old 2.5 voice must still come from the complete July 5 prompt body; the transplant layer only changes the internal task and protects the old draft's viral mechanisms from being softened.

Resolve the two known conflicts after the original block: its `6000-8000` target remains a preferred depth range, while the current hard gate is at least 4000 Chinese characters with no upper limit; its legacy `内部群` ending is overridden by the current exact `粉丝群` ending. ## Validation Gate

Reject and retry when any condition fails:

- fewer than 4000 Chinese characters;
- mother topic changed or diluted;
- promised list count is incomplete;
- an old item was merely renamed or reordered;
- an old signature case, dialogue, or metaphor returns;
- the draft becomes safe psychology education or foregrounds defensive qualifiers;
- the draft exposes structure labels other than the intended spoken list heads;
- no mid-article CTA, no ending CTA, or the fixed final sentence is not exact;
- `内部群`, `私董会`, risk notes, highlights, confirmation language, or backend traces appear.

Before Word export, run a wording pass for invented or awkward expressions. Fix only obvious local defects, such as `性缩力` -> `吸引力会直接崩塌`; do not use local polishing to soften the article or replace its argument.
