## Article-First Design Contract

1. Extract and lock the public mother topic and record any source-promised count from the body. Filename, knowledge-pack title, prior output title, and summary labels have no authority. Treat the source count as reference evidence rather than the final count lock.
2. Record the source fingerprint: audience, relationship stage, promised result, attack direction, source mechanism family, and force band.
3. Apply `transplant-mechanism-novelty-gate.md` against current 3.5 history plus usable 3.1/3.2/3.3 routes for the same source or topic.
4. Codex writes one sentence that states the whole article's dominant causal claim. If this sentence cannot explain why every planned point belongs in the article, rebuild the route.
5. Choose the final public point count and design the complete point hierarchy before retrieval. The count may equal, reduce, or increase the source count; prefer the number supported by the strongest same-level points and the intended speaking rhythm. Every public point must live at the same semantic level. Do not mix stable inner traits, isolated chat tricks, body-language gestures, relationship tactics, moral advice, and abstract psychology under one numbered promise unless the mother topic itself explicitly mixes them. Record why the count changed. After the frozen source is accepted, the selected count becomes binding.
6. Arrange points as a causal or escalating chain. Apply the rearrangement test: if most points can move anywhere without changing the argument, the article is still a card list rather than a designed manuscript.
7. Preserve Gary force through concrete interest movement, relationship-position change, consequence, and judgment. Do not obtain coherence by making the source safer, more neutral, or more educational.

## Knowledge-Support Contract

Retrieve after the article spine exists. The knowledge base is a weapon library, not the article generator.

Allowed knowledge roles:

- explain a mechanism already required by the route;
- provide an observable signal or proof operation;
- supply a topic-matched metaphor, contrast, or memorable judgment;
- strengthen a consequence or interest shift;
- supply a source-backed Gary student case candidate.

Forbidden knowledge behavior:

- using retrieved card titles as public point headings;
- changing the mother topic or point hierarchy to fit available cards;
- adding a locally relevant module that breaks the whole-article causal spine;
- treating the number of matched cards as proof of article quality;
- forcing one knowledge module into every point merely for provenance symmetry.

As a default calibration, let retrieved material support roughly 30%-40% of the frozen source's substantive roles. This is not a sentence-count quota. Codex remains responsible for the article-level thesis, hierarchy, causal bridges, and topic-native ordering.

## Experience-Anchor Contract

When an argument benefits from a concise Gary authority or observation cue, read `3.5-experience-anchor-library.md` and use only its approved wording.

- Preserve the selected expression exactly and replace only its `XX` slots with content supported by the source, selected knowledge, or a confirmed Gary record.
- Treat anchors as optional evidence language, not a fixed density target or recurring point opener.
- Select and complete the sentence before freezing the source. Only the completed sentence enters the frozen source.
- Never append the complete anchor library, its IDs, or another experience-density instruction to Gemini.
- Record selected IDs in `experience_anchor_ids`; record an empty list when no anchor is used.
- Do not treat a generic experience anchor as proof of a concrete outcome. Any claimed student result still needs the full Student Mini-Case evidence fields below.
- Add new expressions to the approved library only after explicit user approval.

## Student Mini-Case Contract

Use one or two cases by default. A case belongs after the theory it proves and must return to that theory before the next point.

Every adopted case needs four internal evidence fields:

1. `student_problem`: the original observable problem;
2. `gary_guidance`: the concrete adjustment made under Gary's guidance or consultation;
3. `observable_result`: the bounded change that was actually recorded;
4. `theory_return`: the exact principle this change proves.

Case-source requirements:

- Prefer a user-provided Gary case document, confirmed consultation record, or a prior manuscript whose case the user has explicitly identified as their student case.
- An external `GCOMP-*` or course case cannot be called a verified Gary student merely because its scene is useful. Use it only as an anonymous composite unless the user separately confirms it is their case.
- Do not invent names, dates, screenshots, consultation volume, money, number of attempts, exact dialogue, guarantees, or a stronger ending than the source records.
- Keep each case compact and subordinate to the argument. Across the full manuscript, cases should normally occupy no more than about 20% of the body.
- Avoid consultation-flip theater. The proof is the before/adjustment/observable-change chain, not Gary scolding the student or claiming a miracle.

If Gemini adds an unsupported case fact, narrow that sentence back to the frozen evidence boundary. Record the exact before/after pair in `case_fact_repairs`; do not keep model-added success details because they sound dramatic.

## Acceptance Gate

Reject or rebuild when:

- the exact source-body mother topic changes, or the selected final count changes after freeze;
- the point list mixes incompatible semantic levels;
- no one-sentence article thesis explains every point;
- most points pass the rearrangement test and behave like independent cards;
- a knowledge-card title becomes a public heading or forces a new umbrella label;
- knowledge is decorative, locally relevant but globally off-route, or repeated only to satisfy a quota;
- the route becomes safer or more neutral than the source;
- a case appears before its theory, creates a second mother topic, or occupies too much of the article;
- a student case lacks the problem, Gary adjustment, observable result, or theory return;
- an external/composite case is presented as a verified Gary student case;
- Gemini invents a case result and the unsupported detail remains in the accepted body;
- the complete old 2.5 prompt is not used, the request is not isolated, or a second instruction layer is appended;
- the fixed natural-rhythm lock is missing, paraphrased, or manually duplicated;
- adjacent points repeat one visible argument shell, every point receives nearly equal depth, or the naturalness target causes mother-topic or point-function drift;
- the final body has fewer than 4,000 Chinese characters, misses the selected final count, exposes production language, or lacks the fixed ending;
- the Word lacks the title package, two generated openings, verbatim source opening, optimized source opening, and正文;
- the deliverable is promoted beyond `generated / 正文待确认` without explicit user confirmation.
