## Canonical Meaning

`body-derived exact mother topic plus source-count reference -> classify the internal content line -> build the same-mother-topic lineage exclusion -> Codex designs and scores three topic-native candidate routes -> select one passing final count, article-level thesis, causal spine, and same-level point hierarchy -> retrieve knowledge only as support -> when useful, insert a named student case with a named woman, Gary intervention, and bounded successful result -> freeze one coherent source -> complete old 2.5 prompt plus fixed 3.5 mother-topic, content-line, selected-count, named-case, and natural-rhythm locks -> dedicated Gary voice candidate-session expansion -> case-fact boundary repair -> post-expansion title-hidden audit -> match one user-supplied popular opening -> two-section Word -> structural and semantic QA -> whole-library final-text similarity check -> promote voice candidate only after user approval`

## Internal Content-Line Routing

Every 3.5 manuscript receives one internal `content_line` before route design:

- `short_term`: the reader wants rapid attraction, early meetings, same-night continuation, flirtation return, physical proximity, removal of practical obstacles, non-exclusive expectations, or private-setting transitions. Express this line through time, setting, distance, choices, observable feedback, and immediate behavior. Do not repeatedly explain that the article is about `短期关系`. If the exact locked mother topic contains that phrase, use it as required in the opening topic sentence and normally do not repeat it elsewhere.
- `growth_psychology`: the reader wants male growth, female psychology, human-nature rules, frame and subjectivity, money/career, or stronger judgment and choice power. This line is not a synonym for couple maintenance. Do not default to cohabitation, conflict repair, shared responsibilities, future planning, or boyfriend/girlfriend operating rules unless the exact mother topic itself requires them. Do not repeatedly call the material `长期关系`; let the psychological mechanism, human behavior, and capability change carry the direction.

The line label is production metadata, not public copy. Do not write `短期线`, `成长/心理线`, `短期向`, `长期向`, or a repeated relationship-category explanation into the title or body. Keep the exact public mother topic authoritative when it naturally contains a relationship term.

For a scheduled sequence or batch without a user-specified order, alternate `short_term -> growth_psychology -> short_term -> growth_psychology`. Continue from the last registered line rather than restarting automatically. A user-selected topic or explicit order overrides the alternation. Adjacent manuscripts must also change the core mechanism, main scene, evidence operation, and closing conclusion even when cross-topic mechanism reuse would otherwise be allowed.

`short_term` examples are direction cues, not a mandatory seven-point template. Do not force rapid scheduling, date extension, flirtation return, proximity, obstacle removal, boundary discussion, and private setting into every article. Select only mechanisms native to the locked mother topic. Adult voluntary interaction may remain a concise background boundary, but do not turn `明确同意` into a numbered public mechanism unless the locked mother topic explicitly asks for consent or boundary recognition.

## Article-First Design Contract

1. Extract and lock the public mother topic and record any source-promised count from the body. Filename, knowledge-pack title, prior output title, and summary labels have no authority. Treat the source count as reference evidence rather than the final count lock.
2. Record the source fingerprint: audience, relationship stage, promised result, attack direction, source mechanism family, and force band.
3. Apply `transplant-mechanism-novelty-gate.md` to the proposed public point hierarchy and dominant causal route against the source body and every earlier route in the same mother-topic/article lineage, regardless of status. Do not apply a global mechanism blacklist across unrelated mother topics. Familiar mechanisms may be reused under a different mother topic when its entry angle, scenes, evidence, causal order, and wording are rebuilt and the final manuscript passes the whole-library text-similarity review.
4. Codex writes one sentence that states the whole article's dominant causal claim. If this sentence cannot explain why every planned point belongs in the article, rebuild the route.
5. Choose the final public point count and design the complete point hierarchy before retrieval. The count may equal, reduce, or increase the source count; prefer the number supported by the strongest same-level points and the intended speaking rhythm. Every public point must live at the same semantic level. Do not mix stable inner traits, isolated chat tricks, body-language gestures, relationship tactics, moral advice, and abstract psychology under one numbered promise unless the mother topic itself explicitly mixes them. Record why the count changed. After the frozen source is accepted, the selected count becomes binding.
6. Arrange points as a causal or escalating chain. Apply the rearrangement test: if most points can move anywhere without changing the argument, the article is still a card list rather than a designed manuscript.
7. Preserve Gary force through concrete interest movement, relationship-position change, consequence, and judgment. Do not obtain coherence by making the source safer, more neutral, or more educational.

### Lean Frozen-Source Contract

The frozen source is a route skeleton for expansion, not a shortened finished article. A normal 3.5 frozen source should usually contain:

- one concise article-level thesis;
- the selected same-level point headings in their final order;
- for each point, its unique judgment, mechanism boundary, one essential scene or proof direction, and the drift it must avoid;
- only the facts and bounded result that an adopted case must preserve;
- the exact marker `此处植入案例。` at the intended location when a new case is delegated to Gemini;
- the fixed ending.

Do not pre-write full rhetorical paragraphs, repeated transitions, complete error-versus-correct demonstrations, multiple finished examples for every point, or a polished final summary. Do not label blocks with editorial headings such as `总判断：`, `收束：`, `开头逻辑：`, `核心总结：`, `写作说明：`, or `段落任务：`; write the underlying judgment as natural narration. The exact `此处植入案例。` marker is the only production placeholder allowed in the frozen source, and it must be replaced before public copy. Leave sentence development, pacing, transitions, most scene detail, and all delegated case detail to Gemini. Keep the frozen source within the builder's adaptive lean limit: `max(1800, selected_count * 150 + 600)` Chinese characters. If the topic needs more support, compress the wording, preserve only the structural facts Gemini cannot infer safely, and let expansion supply the prose.

Before prompt construction, record `frozen_cjk`, `adaptive_frozen_cjk_limit`, and `lean_frozen_source_pass`. A source that exceeds the limit or already reads like a complete publishable manuscript must be compressed before expansion.

### Three-Candidate Codex Selection

Before knowledge retrieval, Codex must create three materially different candidate routes inside the locked mother topic. This is a local design step and must not call Gemini or create three paid expansions.

For each candidate, record:

- one article-level dominant causal claim;
- the proposed final count and same-level public point hierarchy;
- the causal or escalating route signature;
- the shared rule connecting every domain promised by the mother topic;
- the title inferred after hiding the source title;
- at least one reason the candidate may drift, collide, or lack expansion depth.

Score each candidate out of 100:

- mother-topic-native fit: 30;
- route novelty against the source and complete same-mother-topic lineage: 25;
- causal fit across every promised domain: 20;
- expansion capacity without filler: 15;
- natural Gary oral progression: 10.

Select only the highest-scoring candidate that reaches 80 and has no hard failure under the Mother-Topic-Native Replacement Gate. Do not average away a hard failure. If none passes, change the count, combine compatible unused mechanisms, or stop before Gemini. Save all three candidates and rejection reasons. Discarded candidates remain excluded from later design inside that same mother-topic lineage; they do not create a global ban for unrelated topics.

## Mother-Topic-Native Replacement Gate

Complete replacement does not mean permission to write any new article that can be loosely associated with the source. Novelty is subordinate to topic fidelity. Replace only the answers beneath the exact public mother topic; never replace the question, audience desire, promised result, central conflict object, or the reason the audience clicked.

Before knowledge retrieval, test the complete proposed route at both article and point level:

1. **Direct-answer test:** every public point must directly answer the source mother topic in its original sense. A point that is merely related to broad psychology, human nature, relationships, money, status, or self-improvement fails.
2. **Promise-delivery test:** each point must move the same target reader toward the source's promised result. Shared vocabulary or a relationship/business example does not count as delivering the promise.
3. **Native-title test:** hide the source title and ask what title the proposed body naturally demands. If a materially different title is more accurate, the route has become another article and fails.
4. **Decorative-mapping test:** when the mother topic explicitly joins domains such as dating and money, the same causal rule must operate in both domains. Adding one dating example and one business example to an unrelated psychological theory is decorative mapping and fails.
5. **No-new-umbrella test:** do not invent an abstract theory, framework, or umbrella label and then force the source mother topic to serve as its packaging. If the new umbrella explains the body more accurately than the source mother topic, reject the route.
6. **Question-substitution test:** restate the exact reader question implied by the source, then answer it using only the proposed point names. If the answer silently changes what the reader is trying to learn, reject the route.

Treat failure of any test as failure of the entire route. Do not patch only the visibly drifting point. Record the rejected route in history, set `mother_topic_native_pass` to false, and do not retrieve further knowledge, freeze a source, or call Gemini. Passing the route-skeleton novelty screen is necessary but never sufficient for passing this gate.

For the selected candidate, also run an expansion-capacity audit before freezing. Every point must contain or support one judgment, one mechanism, one concrete dating/relationship scene, one concrete business/money scene when the mother topic promises both, one position or interest movement, and one consequence. This is a semantic sufficiency check, not a demand for equal-length sections or a repeated visible template.

## 3.5 Same-Lineage Route Exclusion And Global Final Similarity Contract

For 3.5, replacement means that the public point hierarchy and dominant causal route beneath the locked mother topic must be new within that mother-topic/article lineage. Preserve the public mother topic, audience, promised result, attack direction, and force band. The source count remains adjustable under the count rule. Do not lightly rename, reorder, or recombine the source's or same-lineage history's public points into the new skeleton:

1. **Complete same-mother-topic lineage:** every discoverable 3.1, 3.3, 3.5, transplant, or other Gary rewrite record that answers the same exact or clearly equivalent mother topic, regardless of status or production stage. Include proposed, queued, frozen-source, generated, `正文待确认`, accepted, rejected, abandoned, retry, failed, exported, and published records. A rejected same-lineage route is still excluded for the next version of that mother topic.
2. **Current source layer:** its public answers, dominant mechanism family, causal sequence, signature examples, and distinctive conclusions. The source is used to lock the topic and promise boundary, not as a passage reservoir.
3. **Unrelated-topic layer:** do not exclude a mechanism merely because another mother topic used it. Cross-topic reuse is allowed, but the new article must change its entry angle, scenes, proof operations, causal order, position/interest movement, and wording so it is native to the current topic rather than copied from the other article.

Route newness is semantic, not merely lexical. Changing nouns, examples, order, labels, knowledge IDs, count, or wording does not make an inherited public skeleton new. However, common foundational concepts, generic connective language, unavoidable topic words, and familiar technique vocabulary may appear as secondary explanation when they do not become a numbered answer, the dominant causal bridge, or a substantial reproduction of an earlier passage. Terms such as `沉没成本`, `间歇性强化`, or `推拉` do not fail a draft by themselves.

Before designing the new route:

- create a `source_exclusion_record.json` that lists `source_viewpoints` and `source_mechanisms` from the body;
- create or refresh a `same_topic_lineage_exclusion.json` containing every discoverable same-mother-topic artifact, its status, path, viewpoints, mechanisms, and route signature;
- record which folders, ledgers, and artifacts were searched, plus any unreadable or missing location;
- compare every proposed public point and the dominant route against the source and same-topic lineage exclusion;
- reject the proposed route when its public hierarchy or dominant causal machinery repeats within the lineage. A mechanism found only under an unrelated mother topic is not a failure; record it as cross-topic reuse and rebuild its execution for the current topic.

Run the deterministic screen with the complete same-topic lineage, then perform a semantic audit because wording similarity cannot detect conceptual paraphrase:

```bash
python3 scripts/validate_transplant_mechanism_novelty.py \
  --proposal <proposed-route.json> \
  --ledger <3.5-ledger.json> \
  --ledger <same-topic-lineage-exclusion.json> \
  --exclusion-record <source-exclusion-record.json> \
  --recent 0 \
  --all-statuses \
  --report-out <mechanism-novelty-report.json>
```

Add `--strict-zero-overlap` only when the active user request explicitly requires literal exclusion of every concept. The normal 3.5 rule excludes repeated public skeletons and dominant causal routes while allowing familiar foundational mechanisms as secondary explanation.

Do not freeze or send the manuscript to Gemini unless `deterministic_route_novelty_pass`, `semantic_route_novelty_pass`, and `mother_topic_native_pass` are true. If the available material cannot support a new dominant route inside the locked topic, reduce or increase the point count, combine compatible new mechanisms, or stop and report that no compliant route is available instead of relabeling the old skeleton.

After Gemini returns, run a conservative whole-document comparison against the source body and all discoverable historical Gary manuscript full texts across mother topics and versions. Normalize whitespace and punctuation, exclude only the locked mother-topic phrase and fixed ending, and record at least the strongest matching source, longest exact Chinese-character block, substantial exact-sentence matches, and character-shingle overlap. This is a final-text duplication gate, not a global mechanism blacklist. Reject or revise only when the final draft contains a substantial copied passage, recreates an earlier public point chain, or crosses the project's configured similarity threshold. Do not reject merely because a common concept or short generic phrase appears. This internal gate improves the chance of passing platform deduplication but must not be described as a guarantee of an undisclosed platform algorithm.

## Knowledge-Support Contract

Retrieve after the article spine exists. The knowledge base is a weapon library, not the article generator.

Allowed knowledge roles:

- explain a mechanism already required by the route;
- provide an observable signal or proof operation;
- supply a topic-matched metaphor, contrast, or memorable judgment;
- strengthen a consequence or interest shift;
- supply a source-backed Gary student case candidate.

Forbidden knowledge behavior:

- using retrieved card titles as public point headings;
- changing the mother topic or point hierarchy to fit available cards;
- adding a locally relevant module that breaks the whole-article causal spine;
- treating the number of matched cards as proof of article quality;
- forcing one knowledge module into every point merely for provenance symmetry.

As a default calibration, let retrieved material support roughly 30%-40% of the frozen source's substantive roles. This is not a sentence-count quota. Codex remains responsible for the article-level thesis, hierarchy, causal bridges, and topic-native ordering.

## Student Mini-Case Contract

Use cases only where they help the argument. A case belongs after the theory it proves and must return to that theory before the next point.

Every adopted case needs six internal evidence fields:

1. `student_name`: the male student/brother's name;
2. `woman_name`: the woman's name;
3. `student_problem`: the original observable problem;
4. `gary_guidance`: the concrete adjustment made under Gary's guidance, coaching, or consultation;
5. `successful_result`: the bounded, observable result obtained after the student applied that adjustment;
6. `theory_return`: the exact principle this change proves.

Every adopted case also needs a diversity record:

1. `background_sentence`: the first public sentence after the transition, naturally stating when the man came to Gary, the relationship stage at that moment, and at least one relevant personal detail such as occupation, city, age band, or how they met;
2. `case_structure_signature`: a compact sequence covering the starting state, consultation trigger, Gary intervention, student's changed action, woman's response, and result shape;
3. `recent_case_comparison`: the recent cases checked before freeze;
4. `changed_dimensions`: at least three substantive dimensions changed when a recent skeleton collides;
5. `case_diversity_pass`: whether the case is topic-native and structurally distinct.

Case-source requirements:

- Prefer a user-provided Gary case document, confirmed consultation record, or a prior manuscript whose case the user has explicitly identified as their student case.
- Every public case must name both people. Use a natural male name and a natural female name.
- Place this exact natural transition immediately before every inserted case: `我给兄弟们看一个最近咨询的案例。` Do not add the retired reconstruction disclosure, `（化名）`, or another production-status sentence around it. In the Word package, highlight only the complete transition sentence in yellow.
- The before/after chain is mandatory: named student's original difficulty -> Gary's concrete guidance, coaching, or consultation -> what the student changed -> named woman's observable response -> concrete successful result.
- Immediately after the transition, introduce the man with one natural background sentence. It must say when he came to Gary, how far he and the named woman had progressed, and one relevant personal detail. Prefer natural timing such as `去年年底` or `认识两个月后`; do not fabricate documentary precision.
- Present the successful result directly through topic-native observable behavior: she initiated the next meeting, extended the interaction, increased investment, agreed to the planned next step, or another bounded outcome. Do not comment on how the result was written, compare it with a weaker writing choice, quote an internal rejected example, or explain the case-validation rule inside the public manuscript.
- Do not default every case to `第一次失败 -> 找Gary咨询 -> 第二次见面 -> 女生延长时间 -> 回传内容 -> 给出下次时间`. Before freezing, compare the proposed case structure with recent 3.5 cases. If the skeleton collides, change at least three of: relationship stage, setting, original failure, Gary intervention, student's action, woman's observable response, result shape, and narrative order. Names, surface objects, and isolated dialogue substitutions do not count as changed dimensions.
- Bind the case to the article's actual mechanism. The setting, observable clue, Gary adjustment, and result must prove this manuscript's thesis rather than function as a generic success insert. Rotate result shapes when the topic supports it: creating a shared rule, reversing who initiates, contributing a route, defending shared time, repairing a live misread, bringing another person into the social frame, or maintaining a private language can all be bounded results.
- Do not invent precise dates, exact money, screenshots, consultation volume, guarantees, or claims that an illustrative case was independently verified. Avoid documentary-sounding exact dialogue unless it comes from a confirmed record.
- Keep each case compact and subordinate to the argument. Across the full manuscript, cases should normally occupy no more than about 20% of the body.
- Avoid consultation-flip theater. Gary's intervention must change the student's decision or behavior; the proof comes from the before/guidance/action/response/result chain rather than Gary scolding the student or announcing a miracle.

When the frozen source uses `此处植入案例。`, do not require case names, background, scene, intervention, or result before submission; those are Gemini's expansion responsibility. In the returned manuscript, a case missing either name, the exact transition sentence, the natural background sentence, the Gary intervention, the bounded successful result, or the case-diversity record fails the case gate and must be rejected before acceptance. If Gemini leaves the placeholder visible, omits or changes the transition, restores the retired reconstruction disclosure, collapses the background into a generic `有个兄弟来找我`, turns the result into an unsupported miracle, repeats a recent case skeleton with only names or objects changed, or exposes editorial/QA language such as explaining that a result is not an empty phrase, reject or rerun from the same lean frozen source; do not silently preserve the defective case. Record any factual-boundary repair in `case_fact_repairs`.
