## Article-First Design Contract

1. Extract and lock the public mother topic and record any source-promised count from the body. Filename, knowledge-pack title, prior output title, and summary labels have no authority. Treat the source count as reference evidence rather than the final count lock.
2. Record the source fingerprint: audience, relationship stage, promised result, attack direction, source mechanism family, and force band.
3. Apply `transplant-mechanism-novelty-gate.md` against current 3.5 history and the complete historical 3.1 exclusion corpus described below. This 3.5 override is not limited to recent, usable, same-source, or same-topic records.
4. Codex writes one sentence that states the whole article's dominant causal claim. If this sentence cannot explain why every planned point belongs in the article, rebuild the route.
5. Choose the final public point count and design the complete point hierarchy before retrieval. The count may equal, reduce, or increase the source count; prefer the number supported by the strongest same-level points and the intended speaking rhythm. Every public point must live at the same semantic level. Do not mix stable inner traits, isolated chat tricks, body-language gestures, relationship tactics, moral advice, and abstract psychology under one numbered promise unless the mother topic itself explicitly mixes them. Record why the count changed. After the frozen source is accepted, the selected count becomes binding.
6. Arrange points as a causal or escalating chain. Apply the rearrangement test: if most points can move anywhere without changing the argument, the article is still a card list rather than a designed manuscript.
7. Preserve Gary force through concrete interest movement, relationship-position change, consequence, and judgment. Do not obtain coherence by making the source safer, more neutral, or more educational.

## Mother-Topic-Native Replacement Gate

Complete replacement does not mean permission to write any new article that can be loosely associated with the source. Novelty is subordinate to topic fidelity. Replace only the answers beneath the exact public mother topic; never replace the question, audience desire, promised result, central conflict object, or the reason the audience clicked.

Before knowledge retrieval, test the complete proposed route at both article and point level:

1. **Direct-answer test:** every public point must directly answer the source mother topic in its original sense. A point that is merely related to broad psychology, human nature, relationships, money, status, or self-improvement fails.
2. **Promise-delivery test:** each point must move the same target reader toward the source's promised result. Shared vocabulary or a relationship/business example does not count as delivering the promise.
3. **Native-title test:** hide the source title and ask what title the proposed body naturally demands. If a materially different title is more accurate, the route has become another article and fails.
4. **Decorative-mapping test:** when the mother topic explicitly joins domains such as dating and money, the same causal rule must operate in both domains. Adding one dating example and one business example to an unrelated psychological theory is decorative mapping and fails.
5. **No-new-umbrella test:** do not invent an abstract theory, framework, or umbrella label and then force the source mother topic to serve as its packaging. If the new umbrella explains the body more accurately than the source mother topic, reject the route.
6. **Question-substitution test:** restate the exact reader question implied by the source, then answer it using only the proposed point names. If the answer silently changes what the reader is trying to learn, reject the route.

Treat failure of any test as failure of the entire route. Do not patch only the visibly drifting point. Record the rejected route in history so its mechanisms remain excluded, set `mother_topic_native_pass` to false, and do not retrieve further knowledge, freeze a source, or call Gemini. Passing zero-overlap validation is necessary but never sufficient for passing this gate.

## 3.5 Complete 3.1 And Source Exclusion Contract

For 3.5, replacement means complete replacement beneath the locked public mother topic. Preserve only the public mother topic, audience, promised result, attack direction, and force band. The source count remains adjustable under the count rule. Do not preserve, lightly rewrite, recombine, rename, or use as a supporting subargument any substantive viewpoint or mechanism from either exclusion layer:

1. **Complete historical 3.1 layer:** every discoverable 3.1 record and artifact, regardless of status or production stage. Include proposed, queued, frozen-source, generated, `正文待确认`, accepted, rejected, abandoned, retry, failed, exported, and published records. Search all known 3.1 ledgers plus saved route proposals, frozen sources, run records, and prior Word/text outputs. A rejected route is still excluded; its rejection does not make its mechanism reusable in 3.5.
2. **Current source layer:** every substantive claim, numbered answer, mechanism, causal explanation, judgment, technique logic, and supporting viewpoint present in the source body. The source is used to lock the topic and promise boundary, not as a content reservoir.

“No repetition” is semantic, not lexical. Changing nouns, examples, order, labels, knowledge IDs, count, or wording does not make an inherited viewpoint or mechanism new. A candidate fails when any public point, causal bridge, supporting argument, technique rationale, or conclusion is semantically equivalent to either exclusion layer. Generic connective language and unavoidable words in the locked public topic do not count as substantive repetition.

Before designing the new route:

- create a `source_exclusion_record.json` that lists `source_viewpoints` and `source_mechanisms` from the body;
- create or refresh a consolidated `all_3_1_exclusion_manifest.json` containing every discoverable historical 3.1 artifact, its status, path, viewpoints, and mechanisms; never stop at the latest N records;
- record which folders, ledgers, and artifacts were searched, plus any unreadable or missing location;
- compare every proposed viewpoint and mechanism against both exclusion files;
- reject the entire proposed route if even one substantive overlap remains. Do not keep the non-overlapping points and patch only the collision.

Run the deterministic screen with the complete corpus, then perform a semantic audit because wording similarity cannot detect conceptual paraphrase:

```bash
python3 scripts/validate_transplant_mechanism_novelty.py \
  --proposal <proposed-route.json> \
  --ledger <3.5-ledger.json> \
  --ledger <all-3.1-exclusion-manifest.json> \
  --exclusion-record <source-exclusion-record.json> \
  --recent 0 \
  --all-statuses \
  --strict-zero-overlap \
  --report-out <mechanism-novelty-report.json>
```

Do not freeze or send the manuscript to Gemini unless both `deterministic_zero_overlap_pass` and `semantic_zero_overlap_pass` are true. If the available knowledge cannot support a fully new route inside the locked topic, stop and report that no compliant route is available instead of borrowing from the source or prior 3.1.

## Knowledge-Support Contract

Retrieve after the article spine exists. The knowledge base is a weapon library, not the article generator.

Allowed knowledge roles:

- explain a mechanism already required by the route;
- provide an observable signal or proof operation;
- supply a topic-matched metaphor, contrast, or memorable judgment;
- strengthen a consequence or interest shift;
- supply a source-backed Gary student case candidate.

Forbidden knowledge behavior:

- using retrieved card titles as public point headings;
- changing the mother topic or point hierarchy to fit available cards;
- adding a locally relevant module that breaks the whole-article causal spine;
- treating the number of matched cards as proof of article quality;
- forcing one knowledge module into every point merely for provenance symmetry.

As a default calibration, let retrieved material support roughly 30%-40% of the frozen source's substantive roles. This is not a sentence-count quota. Codex remains responsible for the article-level thesis, hierarchy, causal bridges, and topic-native ordering.

- Preserve the selected expression exactly and replace only its `XX` slots with content supported by the source, selected knowledge, or a confirmed Gary record.
- Treat anchors as optional evidence language, not a fixed density target or recurring point opener.
- Select and complete the sentence before freezing the source. Only the completed sentence enters the frozen source.
- Never append the complete anchor library, its IDs, or another experience-density instruction to Gemini.
- Record selected IDs in `experience_anchor_ids`; record an empty list when no anchor is used.
- Do not treat a generic experience anchor as proof of a concrete outcome. Any claimed student result still needs the full Student Mini-Case evidence fields below.
- Add new expressions to the approved library only after explicit user approval.

## Student Mini-Case Contract

Use one or two cases by default. A case belongs after the theory it proves and must return to that theory before the next point.

Every adopted case needs four internal evidence fields:

1. `student_problem`: the original observable problem;
2. `gary_guidance`: the concrete adjustment made under Gary's guidance or consultation;
3. `observable_result`: the bounded change that was actually recorded;
4. `theory_return`: the exact principle this change proves.

Case-source requirements:

- Prefer a user-provided Gary case document, confirmed consultation record, or a prior manuscript whose case the user has explicitly identified as their student case.
- An external `GCOMP-*` or course case cannot be called a verified Gary student merely because its scene is useful. Use it only as an anonymous composite unless the user separately confirms it is their case.
- Do not invent names, dates, screenshots, consultation volume, money, number of attempts, exact dialogue, guarantees, or a stronger ending than the source records.
- Keep each case compact and subordinate to the argument. Across the full manuscript, cases should normally occupy no more than about 20% of the body.
- Avoid consultation-flip theater. The proof is the before/adjustment/observable-change chain, not Gary scolding the student or claiming a miracle.

If Gemini adds an unsupported case fact, narrow that sentence back to the frozen evidence boundary. Record the exact before/after pair in `case_fact_repairs`; do not keep model-added success details because they sound dramatic.

## Acceptance Gate

Reject or rebuild when:

- the exact source-body mother topic changes, or the selected final count changes after freeze;
- any source-body viewpoint or mechanism remains, including a paraphrase, supporting subargument, causal bridge, technique rationale, or conclusion;
- any viewpoint or mechanism from any discoverable historical 3.1 record remains, regardless of that record's status or production stage;
- the 3.1 exclusion search is limited to recent records, accepted records, the same source/topic, or one ledger;
- either the deterministic or semantic zero-overlap pass is missing or false;
- `mother_topic_native_pass` is missing or false, any point fails direct-answer or promise-delivery review, or the route passes only because the source title was pasted above unrelated content;
- hiding the source title reveals that a materially different title describes the body more accurately;
- a broad psychological, human-nature, relationship, or business theory becomes the real article while the source mother topic survives only as packaging;
- dating and business examples are decorative analogies rather than two expressions of the same causal rule required by the mother topic;
- an invented umbrella label is needed to connect the new route back to the source;
- the point list mixes incompatible semantic levels;
- no one-sentence article thesis explains every point;
- most points pass the rearrangement test and behave like independent cards;
- a knowledge-card title becomes a public heading or forces a new umbrella label;
- knowledge is decorative, locally relevant but globally off-route, or repeated only to satisfy a quota;
- the route becomes safer or more neutral than the source;
- a case appears before its theory, creates a second mother topic, or occupies too much of the article;
- a student case lacks the problem, Gary adjustment, observable result, or theory return;
- an external/composite case is presented as a verified Gary student case;
- Gemini invents a case result and the unsupported detail remains in the accepted body;
- the complete old 2.5 prompt is not used, the request is not isolated, or a second instruction layer is appended;
- the fixed natural-rhythm lock is missing, paraphrased, or manually duplicated;
- adjacent points repeat one visible argument shell, every point receives nearly equal depth, or the naturalness target causes mother-topic or point-function drift;
- the final body has fewer than 4,000 Chinese characters, misses the selected final count, exposes production language, or lacks the fixed ending;
- the Word lacks the title package, two generated openings, verbatim source opening, optimized source opening, and正文;
- the deliverable is promoted beyond `generated / 正文待确认` without explicit user confirmation.
