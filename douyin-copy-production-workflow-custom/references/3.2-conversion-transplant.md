# 3.2 Conversion Transplant / 3.2换芯扩写

Use this mode when the user asks for `3.2`, `3.2换芯`, `3.2换芯扩写`, `成交结构换芯`, or asks to rebuild an approved Gary draft with a traffic hook, conversion-oriented causal structure, and several short cases.

This mode is distinct from:

- `2.5成稿换芯`: prioritizes fixed mother topic, old-2.5 expressive DNA, and a genuinely new body, but does not require the conversion structure below;
- `2.6 Single Case`: uses one complete case through the whole article;
- `3.1 Source Pre-Transplant`: rebuilds a pre-expansion source before routine 2.5;
- `2.9 Fusion Draft`: fuses 2.5 pressure with 2.8 hidden structure and does not use a finished-draft transplant parent by default.

## Fixed Outcome

The intended result is:

`same body-derived mother topic + old-2.5 Gary pressure + genuinely new internal content + conversion-oriented outer structure + several short cases + one fan-group conversion objective`

The article must still feel like a Gary long-form content video. It must not become a sales-page script, low-position consultation monologue, safe relationship lesson, or case compilation.

## Input Boundary

Preferred input is an already-expanded, user-approved Gary/2.5 finished draft. Extract and analyze the body first. The body alone determines the mother topic, audience promise, emotional direction, central mechanism, and any promised count. Filename, folder, Word metadata, package title, and earlier output names are traceability fields only.

If the user supplies only a short pre-expansion source, do not silently treat it as a finished-draft parent. Use `3.1` when the user wants source pre-transplant, or first produce an accepted routine 2.5 draft when the user explicitly wants that draft to become the 3.2 parent.

## Inheritance And Replacement

Preserve:

- the body-derived mother topic and audience promise;
- the approved old-2.5 Gary voice, attack level, rough spoken rhythm, rhetorical questions, analogies, uneven pacing, and abrupt hard conclusions;
- one central source mechanism or distinctive causal bloodline;
- zero to two short signature sentences, each used at most once;
- any list count explicitly promised in the body unless the user allows a change.

Replace:

- the old list items or internal argument order;
- old signature cases, dialogue, metaphors, proof scenes, and scene sequence;
- generic mechanism chains already used in recent 3.2 drafts;
- old CTA wording and old conversion bridge.

Inherited content should occupy about 20%-30% of the content role. New content should occupy about 70%-80%. Every retained mechanism must gain at least two new realities: a new scene, interest movement, relationship consequence, contrary proof, or decision variable.

## Conversion-Oriented Outer Structure

Use this as an invisible macro structure. Do not print these labels in the manuscript:

1. strong-conclusion traffic hook;
2. precise scene-based audience identification;
3. the viewer's common wrong action;
4. dark human-nature, interest, or relationship-power mechanism;
5. concrete cost of continuing the wrong action;
6. numbered judgments, standards, or methods that deliver substantial public value;
7. person-specific variable gap that a generic video cannot honestly decide;
8. one fan-group conversion objective.

The opening must remain broad and provocative enough to carry traffic. Do not begin with a narrow consultation pitch such as `如果你正在冷战第三天`. First give the strong public conclusion; then place the target viewer inside a precise chat, date, attraction, conflict, money, commitment, or long-term relationship scene.

The middle must still be a substantial content body. For list topics, every promised item must contain an independent judgment particle and cannot reuse the same mechanism with new labels. For non-list topics, build three to six distinct causal turns rather than stretching one explanation.

The variable gap must appear only after the public content has delivered a useful general answer. Name two or more topic-specific variables that change the individual diagnosis. Do not deliberately hide the article's central answer merely to force a conversion.

## Short-Case System

Short cases are normal and encouraged in 3.2. They are evidence units, not the article's master plot.

Choose the case layout according to the topic:

- `mechanism-attached cases`: after selected mechanisms, add one compact case showing action, feedback, and consequence;
- `woven cases`: distribute three to six compact cases across the article so that the cases interrupt abstraction and keep the long draft concrete;
- `contrast cases`: use two short parallel scenes to show why superficially similar behavior produces different evaluations;
- `dialogue slices`: use two to five lines of realistic dialogue when the topic is chat, invitation, conflict, boundary, or relationship definition.

For numbered list manuscripts, normally attach a short case or concrete scene to about 40%-70% of the items. Do not mechanically force a case into every item when a direct behavioral comparison is clearer. For a non-list mechanism article, normally use three to five short cases.

Each short case should do at least three things:

1. show a concrete action, sentence, timing choice, or relationship scene;
2. show the other person's observable feedback or changed evaluation;
3. return immediately to Gary's mechanism judgment, consequence, or next-action standard.

Cases must remain compact. They should not grow into a complete consultation arc. Do not default to `man fails -> asks Gary -> follows instructions -> woman comes back -> successful reversal`. Do not create a long protagonist biography, repeated student-success testimonial, or miraculous ending. If the user explicitly asks for one case through the whole article, route to `2.6`, not 3.2.

When a case is not supported by a real supplied record, present it as a typical scene or composite illustration, not as a verified client result. Do not fabricate names, statistics, case volume, payment results, guaranteed outcomes, screenshots, or testimonial claims.

## One Conversion Objective

The only conversion objective is entering the Douyin profile fan group. Do not simultaneously request like, follow, comment keyword, private message, course purchase, and group entry.

The article may contain:

- one non-command mid-article relevance bridge that explains what personal evidence changes the diagnosis;
- one explicit fan-group CTA near the end.

If a mid-article CTA is required by the current project package, it must point to the same fan-group action and must not introduce a second conversion objective. Do not repeat the same CTA paragraph twice.

The final CTA must specify:

- one viewer action: enter the fan group from the profile;
- one topic-specific evidence package to bring;
- one bounded judgment or next-action decision the group will provide.

The group CTA must belong to the article. A chat topic may ask for the latest message sequence, reply timing, and who ended the last interaction; an invitation topic may ask for invitation wording, rescheduling, arrival, and follow-up; a cold-relationship topic may ask for contact frequency, initiative, conflict trigger, and repair behavior. Do not default every article to generic `send your chat record`.

## Attack Level And Expression

Keep the old-2.5 expression band: dark, realistic, interest-sensitive, power-sensitive, conclusion-forward, and willing to offend. Do not rewrite the draft into neutral counseling, healthy-relationship education, boundary preaching, or platform-safety framing.

Do not add visible defensive qualifiers such as `不是操控`, `不是控制`, `不是冷漠`, `不是拿捏`, `看懂不等于拿捏`, or `健康关系才是目的` unless the public mother topic is explicitly a concept distinction.

Pressure must come from concrete actions, evaluations, losses, interest movements, and relationship consequences. Do not replace analysis with repeated words such as `绝对`, `极其`, `王权`, `臣服`, `核武器`, or invented compounds such as `位置稳的男人`.

Keep only the operational boundary: do not provide explicit coercion, unlawful conduct, instructions to ignore refusal, or physical-boundary violations. This boundary stays implicit in ordinary manuscripts and must not become the article's visible framing.

## Prompt Assembly

Use the dedicated 3.2 session:

```bash
./scripts/run_gemini_chat.sh --session 3.2-transplant --prompt-file <prompt.txt> --output-file <expanded.txt>
```

Assemble every request in this exact order:

1. the complete current 2026-06-05 early-B `2.5 Direct Draft` block from `references/gemini-expansion.md`;
2. the complete `【追加任务层：Gary 3.2换芯扩写】` block below;
3. an article-specific 3.2 content card;
4. a limited-inheritance card;
5. old-content and recent-route exclusions;
6. the complete approved finished-draft body inside the style-parent wrapper.

The 3.2 layer supplements rather than replaces the complete 2.5 block. It overrides the early-B block only on these points:

- acceptance requires at least 4000 Chinese characters with no upper limit; 5000-7000 is a preferred depth range, not a hard cap;
- use the 3.2 invisible conversion structure;
- short cases are normal evidence units, while one complete case through the article is forbidden unless the user switches to 2.6;
- the one conversion objective is the profile fan group;
- the current exact fixed ending is `我是探花Gary，我们粉丝群里见，感谢观看`;
- `内部群` and `私董会` are forbidden.

## Validation Gate

Reject and rerun from the full prompt when any condition fails:

- fewer than 4000 Chinese characters;
- mother topic, audience promise, emotional direction, or body-promised count changes;
- old items are renamed, reordered, or lightly paraphrased;
- inherited material exceeds its limited 20%-30% role, or the selected core bloodline disappears;
- the opening becomes a narrow consultation pitch instead of a strong public conclusion;
- the article is mostly pain description, marketing, or group promotion and lacks a substantial content middle;
- the invisible conversion structure is exposed as course labels or backend wording;
- no concrete wrong action, dark mechanism, real cost, useful public judgment, or person-specific variable gap appears;
- a numbered promise is not fully delivered or multiple items reuse the same mechanism;
- there are no short cases/concrete evidence scenes, or cases become detached stories that do not prove the current point;
- a short case grows into the forbidden full consultation-reversal template;
- a fabricated case is presented as a verified student/client result;
- fewer than two topic-specific individual variables are named before conversion;
- the CTA has more than one conversion objective, uses generic evidence, or promises an unbounded service;
- the CTA appears before useful public value is delivered;
- the draft becomes safe counseling or foregrounds defensive qualifiers;
- serial-production/backstage words, `内部群`, `私董会`, risk notes, confirmation language, or prompt traces appear;
- the exact fixed final sentence is missing or has text after it.

For batches larger than three, produce and validate in groups of three. Each article uses its own full prompt request through the dedicated 3.2 session. Maintain separate 3.2 route, case-layout, conversion-signature, similarity, and retry ledgers so 3.2 cannot reuse the same scene pool or CTA across the batch.

## Test And Word Output

For a test, export one clean `3.2正文待确认` Word document with the mother-topic title, a short metadata line containing the new skeleton, case layout, and Chinese-character count, then `正文` and the accepted body. Do not add title packaging or three opening versions unless the user requests formal packaging.

For formal production, follow the normal Word packaging and export rules in the parent skill. Keep 3.2 working prompts, accepted bodies, ledgers, and retry records in a separate `_work/` folder inside the delivery batch.
