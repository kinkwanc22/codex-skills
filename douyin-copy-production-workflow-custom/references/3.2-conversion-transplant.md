# 3.2 Case-Guided Source Pre-Transplant / 3.2带小案例换芯扩写

Use this mode when the user asks for `3.2`, `3.2换芯`, `3.2换芯扩写`, `带小案例的预换芯`, or wants the complete 3.1 pre-transplant method with normal short cases added to the rebuilt source and final long draft.

## Canonical Definition

3.2 is a copied-and-modified 3.1 workflow. Its fixed result is:

`body-derived mother topic -> topic fingerprint -> new source-level skeleton -> short-case plan embedded in the rebuilt source -> topic-native terms and conversion handoff -> freeze -> complete standard 2.5 Direct Draft through --isolated -> case-aware validation`

The difference from 3.1 is the case system and the conversion architecture described below, not a different Gemini persona or a second prompt layer.

The difference from `2.5成稿换芯` is that 3.2 does not send an approved finished draft as a style parent and does not use the copied old transplant conversation.

The difference from `2.6 Single Case` is that 3.2 uses several compact evidence cases, contrasts, or dialogue slices. It does not use one protagonist or one consultation event through the whole article.

1. Extract the supplied body. Treat filenames, parent folders, Word properties, stale headings, earlier output titles, and metadata as traceability only.
2. Infer the exact mother topic, audience, relationship stage, emotional direction, promised result, central object, and any spoken count from the body alone.
3. Create a body-derived topic fingerprint before inventing replacement points.
4. Inspect the last 20 accepted 3.2 entries across all titles and avoid saturated mechanism families, repeated route signatures, repeated scene chains, and repeated conversion signatures.
5. Keep the mother topic and body-promised count, but replace the internal points, proof order, examples, signals, scenes, and mechanism sequence with genuinely different content.
6. Select a topic-native primary mechanism family. Do not default unrelated topics to `框架 -> 投入 -> 损失厌恶`.
7. Apply the title-transfer test. Every key point must depend on this topic's actual object, action, scene, and consequence.
8. Select 2-5 total topic-matched terms across psychology, relationship-game/PUA, sociology, or biology. Embed them naturally in the rebuilt source and demonstrate them through nearby behavior or consequences.
9. Choose one topic-native fan-group conversion handoff. Define the individual variables, evidence package, and bounded decision before writing the source.
10. Start the rebuilt source directly from the new thesis. Never summarize, mock, downgrade, or argue against the old source points before presenting the new content.
11. Make the rebuilt source read like a natural short Gary manuscript, not a prompt, outline, content card, or course module.
12. Freeze the rebuilt source as the only expansion source of truth and record the route before calling Gemini.
13. Send the frozen source through the complete standard 2.5 block with `--isolated` and no second layer.
14. Preserve the returned old-2.5 pressure by default. Do not post-edit it into a calmer, safer, more professional, or more neutral relationship lesson.
15. Recheck the final long body against the fingerprint, planned route, case plan, recent ledger, conversion signature, length, count, and ending before export.

All current 3.1 source constraints remain active unless the case rules below explicitly modify them. In particular:

## 3.2 Short-Case Modification

Short cases are designed before expansion and written into the rebuilt source itself. They are content evidence, not instructions sent separately to Gemini.

Choose one or combine two of these layouts:

- `mechanism-attached`: a compact case after selected mechanisms;
- `woven`: three to six compact cases distributed through the source;
- `contrast`: two nearby scenes showing why similar surface behavior creates different evaluations;
- `dialogue slice`: two to five realistic lines for chat, invitation, conflict, testing, boundary, or relationship-definition topics.

Default density:

- numbered/list source: give about 40%-70% of the new points one short case, concrete scene, behavioral comparison, or dialogue slice;
- non-list mechanism source: plan three to five compact cases across distinct causal turns;
- do not mechanically force a case into every point when a direct action-feedback comparison is stronger.

Every short case must contain:

1. a topic-specific action, sentence, timing choice, or relationship scene;
2. observable feedback, changed participation, changed evaluation, or changed interest position;
3. an immediate return to Gary's mechanism judgment, reality cost, or next-action standard.

Cases must be compact. Normally one to three short paragraphs are enough. Do not let a case acquire a long biography, consultation history, repeated coaching steps, or miracle ending.

Forbidden case patterns:

- one man fails, contacts Gary, follows instructions, the woman becomes active, and the relationship flips;
- one protagonist or one consultation event occupying most of the article;
- cases copied or lightly paraphrased from the supplied source;
- the same party, coffee, late-night chat, refusal, and reversal sequence reused across unrelated titles;
- generic cases that could move under another title after changing only the pronouns;
- fabricated student/client cases presented as verified outcomes.

If a case is not supported by a supplied real record, write it as a typical scene, behavioral comparison, or composite illustration. Do not call it a verified student result.

If the user asks for one complete case through the whole article, stop using 3.2 and route to `2.6 Single Case`.

## 3.2 Monetization Architecture Override

3.2 inherits the complete 3.1 pre-transplant architecture, but replaces 3.1's ordinary conversion placement and dual-CTA expectation with this single-CTA monetization architecture. This is the only conversion override; all other inherited 3.1 rules remain active.

Build the rebuilt source around this hidden sequence:

1. a strong-conclusion traffic hook that preserves broad stopping power;
2. precise audience and relationship-scene identification;
3. the concrete wrong action the viewer is likely taking;
4. the topic-native dark mechanism or reality behind the problem;
5. the observable cost of continuing the wrong action;
6. substantial numbered judgments, distinctions, or methods that deliver the public answer;
7. evidence and objection handling, followed by the person-specific variable gap;
8. one final fan-group CTA.

Do not expose labels such as `圈人群`, `痛点`, `放大后果`, `展示方案`, `破顾虑`, `营销`, `CTA`, or `成交结构` in the manuscript. The sequence must read as one continuous Gary argument rather than a sales template.

The traffic hook must lead with a public conclusion, conflict, or result. Do not reduce the opening to a narrow customer-screening sentence. Move into the precise viewer scene immediately after the hook so traffic and conversion work in sequence instead of competing.

The article must deliver a useful general answer before conversion. Do not withhold the promised list, mechanism, distinction, or method merely to force group entry. Cases, contrasts, and dialogue slices belong inside the explanatory and method sections as evidence; they must not become testimonials pasted after the advice.

Evidence and objection handling may use:

- compact typical scenes;
- observable behavior and response changes;
- positive/negative scene contrasts;
- supplied real records when their status is known;
- two or more topic-specific variables showing why the same surface signal can require a different judgment.

Do not fabricate client/student outcomes, screenshot claims, transaction results, or guaranteed reversals. A general article may explain the shared rule completely while reserving one individualized stage judgment or next-action choice that genuinely depends on the viewer's own evidence.

There is only one explicit conversion action in the whole article: enter the Douyin profile fan group near the end. Do not place an explicit group invitation in the middle. Do not simultaneously ask for likes, follows, comments, direct messages, a course purchase, or another destination.

Treat the final conversion paragraph and the fixed Gary sign-off as one ending conversion unit. The paragraph immediately before the fixed sign-off must specify:

- one topic-specific evidence package the viewer should bring;
- one bounded judgment or next-action decision the group helps clarify;
- no promise of a full private consultation, guaranteed result, or unlimited service.

The exact final sentence remains:

`我是探花Gary，我们粉丝群里见，感谢观看`

## Rebuilt Source Contract

Before freezing the new source, it must already contain:

- the exact body-derived mother topic and audience promise;
- a genuinely new topic-native skeleton;
- the body-promised count, if any, fully represented;
- scene anchors for every key point;
- the selected 2-5 terms used naturally;
- the planned short cases written as ordinary source content;
- at least two topic-specific variables that change an individual diagnosis;
- the complete public answer delivered before conversion;
- one final conversion unit only, containing one concrete viewer action, one topic-specific evidence package, and one bounded group-delivered decision;
- enough source detail for the standard 2.5 prompt to expand without any supplemental instruction.

Do not freeze an outline containing labels such as `案例场景池`, `错误动作`, `核心机制`, `现实代价`, `个体变量`, `CTA`, or `扩写要求`. Convert every planning field into natural source prose first.

## Validation Gate

Apply every current 3.1 validation check, replacing the 3.1 ledger path with the 3.2 ledger path, and also reject when:

- no frozen rebuilt source exists before the Gemini call;
- the Gemini prompt contains a 3.2 task layer, case card, style parent, exclusion card, or any supplement beyond complete 2.5 plus frozen source;
- the expansion did not use `--isolated`;
- the source or final body lacks the planned number of short cases or concrete evidence scenes;
- cases do not show action/dialogue, feedback/evaluation, and a return to the current mechanism;
- cases are generic, copied from the supplied source, detached from the current point, or presented as verified results without evidence;
- the final body collapses into one full consultation-reversal story;
- the final body changes the mother topic, emotional direction, audience promise, or body-promised count;
- the source or final body merely renames, reorders, or lightly paraphrases the old points;
- topic terms appear as detached labels or exceed the inherited 2-5 total density;
- the public explanation is withheld to manufacture conversion pressure;
- an explicit fan-group CTA appears before the ending conversion unit;
- more than one conversion action is requested, including any mixture of group entry, likes, follows, comments, direct messages, course purchase, or another destination;
- the CTA is generic, interchangeable with another topic, appears before the required public value and variables, or fails to specify evidence and a bounded decision;
- the manuscript exposes the hidden monetization labels or reads like a sales-page template instead of a continuous Gary argument;
- fewer than 4500 Chinese characters after expansion. Target about 5000-7000 Chinese characters; this is a depth preference, not an upper limit;
- `内部群`, `私董会`, `[[RISKNOTE:...]]`, prompt traces, confirmation language, or serial-production wording appears;
- the exact final sentence is missing or has text after it: `我是探花Gary，我们粉丝群里见，感谢观看`.

When a case requirement fails, first check the frozen source. If the source lacks a strong enough case, rebuild and freeze the source again. If the source is correct but Gemini dropped or distorted it, rerun the same complete 2.5 prompt plus frozen source through `--isolated`. Do not fix the problem by attaching a case supplement.
