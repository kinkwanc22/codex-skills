# 3.1 Balanced Soft Fan-Group Layer

Read this file after a 3.1 Gemini body passes topic, count, force, and length checks and before title/opening packaging or Word export.

## Purpose

The user's conversion path is:

`video traffic -> follow -> Douyin fan group -> private-domain conversion`

The 3.1 manuscript must remain a valuable traffic manuscript first. Gemini is allowed to produce the conversion language required by the complete old 2.5 prompt, but that response is preserved only as the raw artifact. Codex first creates a separate cleaned zero-CTA body by exact, manifested deletion. The fan-group layer then makes Gary's real diagnostic and review capability repeatedly visible and adds the final conversion units after expansion. This is a narrow Codex post-generation edit. It does not change the knowledge route, mother topic, promised count, Gary force band, or old-2.5 expansion style.

## Default Transformation

Unless the user explicitly requests another CTA treatment:

1. Run Gemini with the complete standard 2.5 block plus the fixed mother-topic exact-lock block. Do not suppress or rewrite the old 2.5 conversion instructions.
2. Save the raw Gemini response unchanged. Run the exact conversion cleaner and save a deletion manifest containing every removed unit verbatim and its category/location.
3. Validate the separate cleaned body for zero residual CTA, topic/count, force, and length. Retry only when conversion cannot be isolated without substantive damage or another content gate fails.
4. After the cleaned body is accepted, Codex adds balanced article-native soft placements.
5. In the same Codex pass, add one compact topic-native mid-article CTA after the useful mechanism and diagnostic variables are established, normally around 45%-60% of the body.
6. Codex appends the exact final sentence: `我是探花Gary，我们粉丝群里见，感谢观看`. Do not add a second dynamic ending CTA unless the user explicitly requests it.
7. Save every Codex-added soft placement, mid CTA, and fixed ending in an insertion manifest for Word highlighting and unified validation.

Do not send this layer back to Gemini. Codex performs it after the body is accepted.

## Density

Choose density from the accepted body length, not from a fixed paragraph count:

- Target roughly one soft placement per 600-800 Chinese characters.
- A body around 4000 CJK normally receives 6 placements.
- A body around 6000 CJK normally receives 8-9 placements.
- A body around 8000 CJK normally receives 9-10 placements.
- Do not exceed 10 without explicit user instruction.

For the approved 6095-CJK calibration manuscript, the accepted target is 9 placements.

## Placement Mix

For a 6000-CJK manuscript with 9 soft placements plus one mid CTA:

- 3-4 may explicitly use `粉丝群` or `群里`.
- The remainder should show Gary's diagnostic ability, review method, pattern recognition, or the gap between knowing a theory and locating one's own failure.
- Soft placements do not directly instruct the viewer to `进群`, `关注`, `去主页`, `加入`, or `私信`. The separate default mid CTA contains one concrete viewer action, one topic-specific evidence package, and one bounded group-delivered diagnosis or next-step decision.

Vary the carrier across the manuscript:

- real-interaction review habit;
- diagnostic question or distinction;
- timeline or behavior-chain method;
- common failure pattern seen in submitted interactions;
- occasional fan-group discussion context.

Do not make every placement say `粉丝群里……`, `我在群里……`, `我平时拆……`, or `复盘时……`. Repeating one carrier creates a new advertising template even when each sentence is short.

## Integration

- Write every placement from the current paragraph's mechanism, scene, and judgment. Generic group copy is a failure.
- Mix paragraph-middle, paragraph-end, and transition positions. Do not always append a standalone paragraph after a list item.
- Keep each placement to one natural sentence. It should sound like a continuation of Gary's current explanation.
- A placement may demonstrate expertise or reveal an individual diagnostic gap, but it may not fabricate a named student, result, screenshot, volume claim, or verified group event.
- Do not add a new mechanism, case, operation, conclusion, or promised result. This layer changes conversion visibility only.
- Do not soften, professionalize, or add defensive language around the original claim.
- The mid CTA must be written from this article's exact mechanism. It must not be a generic `有问题进群` paragraph and must not withhold the public answer.
- Keep the mid CTA compact: normally 70-140 Chinese characters in one paragraph. It may be longer only when the evidence package and delivered decision cannot be stated naturally within that range.

## Word Highlight Output

- Default 3.1 Word: highlight every Codex-added soft placement, the mid CTA, and the exact fixed ending in yellow.
- Never highlight source text, Gemini-expanded body text, headings, opening candidates, title packaging, risk notes, or backstage labels.
- Save a JSON insertion manifest with `soft_placements`, `mid_cta`, `fixed_ending`, and `highlight_texts`. `highlight_texts` must be the exact ordered list written as yellow runs in Word.
- If the user explicitly asks for a clean unhighlighted production copy, preserve the highlighted default and export the clean copy separately; validate it with `--allow-no-yellow`.
- Preserve three separate artifacts: immutable Gemini raw, cleaned zero-CTA Gemini body, and Codex conversion-layer final body. Never overwrite one with another.

Manifest shape:

```json
{
  "soft_placements": ["<soft 1>", "<soft 2>"],
  "mid_cta": "<one topic-native CTA>",
  "fixed_ending": "我是探花Gary，我们粉丝群里见，感谢观看",
  "highlight_texts": ["<soft 1>", "<mid CTA at its document position>", "<soft 2>", "我是探花Gary，我们粉丝群里见，感谢观看"]
}
```

`highlight_texts` follows Word-body order, so the mid CTA is interleaved with the soft placements at its actual position rather than always appended after all of them.

## Acceptance Gate

Before export, record and verify:

- `soft_layer_applied: true`
- `body_cjk`
- `soft_placement_count`
- `average_soft_gap_cjk`
- `explicit_fan_group_placement_count`
- `gemini_raw_preserved: true`
- `gemini_raw_conversion_removed: true`
- `gemini_removal_count`
- `gemini_removal_manifest`
- `gemini_clean_cta_free_pass: true`
- `mid_cta_count: 1` by default
- `mid_cta_position`
- `direct_action_placement_count: 1` by default, located only in the mid CTA
- `fixed_ending_preserved: true`
- `highlight_text_count`
- `default_yellow_highlight_applied: true`

Reject and revise when:

- the raw Gemini response is overwritten or its conversion deletions are not recorded verbatim;
- conversion cannot be separated from substantive content without damaging the argument;
- the cleaned Gemini body contains any residual CTA, fan-group invitation, account self-introduction, or fixed ending;
- the Codex mid CTA is missing, generic, repeated, or becomes a large promotional block;
- more than half of the placements explicitly mention the group;
- the same self-reference carrier repeats mechanically;
- the placement can be pasted into an unrelated article unchanged;
- the insertion interrupts the argument, changes the mother topic, or introduces a new claim;
- any yellow text is not present in the insertion manifest, or any manifest text is not yellow in Word;
- source/Gemini text is highlighted;
- any of the raw, cleaned, or conversion-layer artifacts overwrites another.
