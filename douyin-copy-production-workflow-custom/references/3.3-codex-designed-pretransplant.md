# 3.3 Codex-Designed Source Pre-Transplant

Read this file in full whenever the user asks for `3.3`, `3.3独立换芯`, `旧版3.1`, `接入知识库前的3.1`, `让Codex设计新观点`, or another unambiguous equivalent.

## Canonical Meaning

3.3 is the former pre-knowledge-base 3.1 workflow under a new formal version number:

`body-derived exact mother topic and count -> Codex independently designs a genuinely new source-level skeleton and topic-native conversion route -> separate 3.3 route exclusion -> freeze short source -> complete old 2.5 prompt plus fixed 3.3 mother-topic lock -> isolated Gemini expansion -> narrow mechanical acceptance repair -> three-opening Word`

The version boundary is strict:

- 3.3 does not query or cite the Gary knowledge base. Codex content invention is intentional.
- 3.3 does not use 3.1 provenance, conversion cleanup, balanced soft insertion, yellow highlights, four openings, or `validate_31_final_package.py`.
- 3.3 does not change routine 2.5, formal knowledge-base 3.1, or controlled 3.2.
- `2.5预换芯` and `原稿预换芯` without an explicit 3.3/old-mode instruction still route to formal 3.1.

## Workflow

1. Extract the actual source body. Ignore filename, folder name, Word core properties, stale headings, previous outputs, and model summaries while inferring the topic.
2. Copy a verbatim source-body quote and lock the shortest exact wording that still expresses the public mother topic. Lock any count explicitly promised by the body. Filename-only counts have no authority.
3. Record a topic fingerprint: audience, relationship stage, promised result, central object, emotional direction, usable scenes, and source mechanism family to avoid lightly renaming.
4. Inspect the separate 3.3 ledger with `scripts/inspect_33_recent_routes.py`. Exclude prior same-source points, exact route signatures in the last 20 accepted 3.3 entries, and mechanism families already used twice in the last 10 unless the topic inherently requires one and the exception is recorded.
5. Codex independently designs a short replacement source. Keep the exact public mother topic, audience promise, force band, target result, and promised count, but replace the internal points, mechanisms, proof scenes, examples, order, concrete signals, and causal progression. General model reasoning is allowed by design; knowledge IDs and fabricated provenance are forbidden.
6. Start directly with the new thesis and new points. Do not quote, summarize, restate, mock, downgrade, or criticize the source points as setup. The old source is an internal exclusion reference, not a rhetorical opponent.
7. Make every replacement point topic-native. Each point needs a concrete scene anchor, action or observable signal, interest/position movement, and consequence. A point that can move under an unrelated title by changing only pronouns is too generic.
8. Naturally embed 2-5 total topic-matched terms across psychology, PUA, sociology, or biology. The total is across all fields, not per field. Every term must explain a nearby scene or consequence; do not stack detached labels or fabricate studies, statistics, hormones, neural claims, or deterministic biology.
9. Design one topic-native conversion handoff inside the rebuilt source. Complete the useful public explanation first, then show at least two topic-specific variables that can change an individual judgment. Specify one viewer action, a concrete evidence package, and one bounded decision delivered in the fan group. Include one natural mid-article CTA and one concise ending decision CTA without withholding the article's main answer.
10. Freeze the rebuilt source and record the route before Gemini. Run `scripts/validate_mother_topic_lock.py` and confirm the exact anchor, public wording, and count survive. Record `mode: 3.3_codex_designed_pretransplant`, `knowledge_base_used: false`, and `codex_content_invention: true`.
11. Build the request only with `scripts/build_33_gemini_prompt.py`: complete old `2.5 Direct Draft` block + fixed 3.3 mother-topic lock + frozen source. Run one fresh `--isolated` request per source. Do not append knowledge closure, route notes, `旧2.5风格参照`, safety supplements, article-specific execution supplements, or another CTA prompt.
12. Preserve Gemini's old-2.5 hard, realistic, dark language surface. Do not run the 3.1 conversion cleaner or rewrite it into neutral education. Local cleanup is limited to unmistakable typos, isolated backstage leakage, exact mother-topic/count repair, CTA/fixed-ending repair, forbidden group-name replacement, or a minimal operational-boundary correction.
13. Validate the expanded body, then generate titles and the normal three openings. Export a clean Word with no default yellow highlights. Keep state at `generated / 正文待确认` until the user explicitly confirms it.

## Route And Content Rules

- Preserve the original attack direction. Do not replace a sharp promise with neutral abstractions such as `关系治理`, `边界管理`, `健康沟通`, `风险识别`, or another umbrella label.
- A numbered source must keep the same body-promised number while replacing its recognition dimensions, examples, scene order, and proof chain.
- Choose the primary mechanism family from the topic: perception/attention, language/meaning, timing/sequence, environment/context, identity/role, emotion/memory, learning/conditioning, incentives/exchange, social structure, or competence/behavior.
- Treat `框架`, `投入`, `损失厌恶`, `沉没成本`, `关系定价`, and `预选效应` as optional tools, not the default chain. Unless the topic directly concerns cost, withdrawal, investment, or status selection, `框架 -> 投入 -> 损失厌恶` may support at most one secondary point.
- Preserve memorable Gary judgments, interest calculations, selection rights, relationship position changes, emotional reward/punishment, loss anxiety, and concrete consequences when they fit the source. Do not add visible defensive qualifiers merely to make the draft safer.
- Pressure must come from mechanism, scene, interest movement, and consequence, not repetitive danger words or theatrical labels that replace analysis.
- Do not expose production language such as `原稿`, `旧稿`, `换芯`, `版本`, `路线`, `提示词`, `选题不变`, `这一次我们不讲`, or `上一版` in the frozen source or final body.
- Do not use source-derived `不是A而是B`, `很多男人以为`, `情感博主都在教`, `太廉价`, or `太低级` as a disguised way to criticize the source. State the new point directly.

## Conversion Behavior

3.3 preserves the old pre-knowledge-base behavior rather than current 3.1 post-processing:

- The frozen source already contains the article-native conversion logic.
- The complete old 2.5 prompt remains unchanged and Gemini generates its own conversion language and old fixed ending.
- Do not automatically delete Gemini CTA, account language, or fixed ending.
- Do not add the 3.1 balanced soft layer after expansion and do not yellow-highlight conversion text by default.
- If the returned CTA becomes generic, repair it narrowly so it requests topic-specific evidence and delivers one bounded stage judgment or next action. If generic conversion controls a whole section, rerun from the frozen source instead of locally rewriting the argument.

## Acceptance Gate

Reject and rebuild or retry when any of these fail:

- exact source-body mother topic is absent, broadened, or replaced by an umbrella label;
- body-promised count is incomplete or a filename-only count was imposed;
- replacement points are near-synonyms, reordered source points, or generic Gary material;
- the source or final draft criticizes the old source before presenting new points;
- fewer than 2 or more than 5 naturally embedded matched terms appear in the frozen source;
- route signature repeats in the last 20 accepted 3.3 entries or the primary family is saturated without a recorded topic-native exception;
- the frozen source is topic-specific but Gemini collapses it into a recently repeated generic route;
- final body contains backstage leakage, refusal text, stale-topic contamination, or forced identity prefixes such as `作为探花Gary`;
- final body has fewer than 4500 Chinese characters; target roughly 5000, and accept a complete 4500-6000-character body without retrying merely to reach 7000;
- required mid-article and ending fan-group CTAs are missing, interchangeable with unrelated topics, or promise no bounded diagnosis/decision;
- exact fixed ending required by the active old 2.5 project is missing;
- Word lacks the title package, three required openings, or正文, or contains `[[RISKNOTE]]`/risk-note text.

Use a 3.3 output name such as `编号_正文母题_3.3独立换芯_正文待确认.docx`. File existence proves only `generated`; it does not prove user confirmation, export to platform, or publication.
