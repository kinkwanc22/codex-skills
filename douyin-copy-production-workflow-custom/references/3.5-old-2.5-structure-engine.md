# 3.5 Old-2.5 Structure Engine

Read this file during every 3.5 route-selection and frozen-source step. It preserves the transferable strengths found across the approved old-2.5 reference manuscripts without copying their repeated openings, CTA shell, unsupported claims, or topic-specific tactics.

## What 3.5 Inherits

The inherited engine is structural:

`hidden-rule reveal -> reject the reader's old explanation -> governing judgment -> causal explanation -> varied proof -> position or identity shift -> stronger next layer`

Do not imitate old 2.5 by adding more `极其`, `绝对`, `残酷`, `别人不敢教`, `完整未删减版`, age filters, biological certainty, fabricated studies, fixed consultation CTA, or demeaning labels. Those are surface devices and repeated boilerplate. Keep only language that is native to the current mother topic and supported by the frozen route.

## Required Selected-Route Plan

After one route wins the three-candidate selection and before knowledge retrieval, save `old_25_engine_plan.json`. The plan is backstage metadata and must not appear in public copy. `scripts/build_35_gemini_prompt.py` validates it through `--engine-plan` before it can build the request.

Use this schema:

```json
{
  "profile": "old_2.5_structure_engine_v1",
  "public_topic": "<exact locked public topic>",
  "master_memory_spine": {
    "mode": "metaphor|recurring_causal_image",
    "anchor": "<one topic-native sentence that can recur without becoming a new umbrella>",
    "topic_native": true
  },
  "causal_acts": [
    {
      "name": "<backstage act name>",
      "point_ids": [1, 2],
      "old_belief": "<the mistaken explanation challenged in this act>",
      "escalation_role": "<why this act is deeper or closer to the promised result>",
      "transition_sentence": "<natural spoken sentence already present in the frozen source>"
    }
  ],
  "points": [
    {
      "id": 1,
      "heading": "<final public point heading already present in the frozen source>",
      "governing_judgment": "<one contrarian judgment already present in the frozen source>",
      "proof_carrier": "scene|contrast|analogy|case|experience|social_observation|mechanism",
      "causal_link_to_next": "<why the next point follows; the last point may be empty>"
    }
  ],
  "identity_shift": {
    "from": "<losing position or identity>",
    "to": "<winning position or identity>",
    "anchor": "<one natural sentence already present in the frozen source>"
  },
  "rearrangement_test_pass": true,
  "surface_template_reuse": false
}
```

For four or more public points, organize them into two to four causal acts. For one to three points, one to three acts are enough. Every point belongs to exactly one act, point order stays intact, and act order must explain an escalation. If most points can still move freely after grouping, rebuild the route.

The master memory spine is one recurring topic-native image or causal phrase. It may be a metaphor such as `身体泄密` when the mother topic supports it, or a recurring causal image such as `把评价权交出去`. It must clarify the locked mother topic rather than replace it with a new theory. Use one spine, not a new metaphor for every point.

Every point keeps a concise public heading, but its first governing sentence must be a judgment that changes how the reader interprets the behavior. The action, signal, technique, or scene proves that judgment; it is not the whole argument by itself.

Rotate proof carriers across adjacent points. A manuscript may use a concrete scene, high-low contrast, ordinary-life analogy, named Gary case, experience judgment, social observation, or mechanism explanation. Do not force every point through the same visible `wrong behavior -> attack -> psychology -> correct action -> woman feedback` shell. The plan needs at least two distinct carriers, and normally three when the manuscript has three or more points.

The identity shift names the article's movement in plain terms: for example, `waiting to be scored -> keeping one's own pace`. It is not permission to scatter insults through the manuscript. The final body should make the reader feel this movement through consequences and choices.

## Frozen-Source Translation

The JSON remains backstage. Translate it into the lean frozen source as natural narration:

- include the master-spine anchor once near the thesis and allow later development without mechanically repeating the same sentence;
- include every act's natural transition sentence in its correct position;
- include every point heading and its governing judgment;
- give each point only one essential proof direction and one causal bridge;
- place a delegated case marker only where that proof carrier belongs;
- include the identity-shift anchor without writing a polished final summary.

Keep the existing adaptive lean limit. Do not turn the plan into a finished article, label the public text with `总判断`, `因果层级`, `证明载体`, `身份位移`, or paste the JSON into the frozen source.

## Acceptance Check

After Gemini returns, check the body with its title hidden:

- the master memory spine still organizes more than the opening;
- later acts feel deeper or closer to the promised result than earlier acts;
- the numbered points remain judgments supported by actions rather than a flat behavior inventory;
- adjacent points do not repeat one proof shell;
- the reader's position or identity visibly changes by the close;
- no old-2.5 boilerplate or unsupported authority claim was introduced to simulate force.

When the structure fails, reject the body and change the route or frozen source. Do not repair the accepted prose locally by adding harsher adjectives or extra theory.
