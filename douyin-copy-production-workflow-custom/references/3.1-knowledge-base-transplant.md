Read this file in full whenever the user asks for `3.1`, `3.1换芯`, `3.1扩写`, `2.5预换芯`, `原稿预换芯`, or asks to reuse the approved knowledge-base-assisted test.

## Canonical Meaning

The default 3.1 workflow is now:

`body-derived mother topic -> knowledge-base retrieval and history exclusion -> Codex selection/polish/transplant without content invention -> freeze short source -> complete standard 2.5 prompt plus fixed mother-topic exact-lock block -> isolated Gemini expansion -> save raw -> preserve all Gemini content -> normalize only the terminal fixed ending -> exact retention-manifest Word`

This replaces the former default in which Codex independently invented the replacement viewpoints. That former workflow is now formal version `3.3`; route `旧版3.1`, `接入知识库前的3.1`, `让Codex设计新观点`, and equivalent requests to `references/3.3-codex-designed-pretransplant.md`. Never execute it as a hidden 3.1 fallback.

This update changes 3.1 only. Do not silently impose Codex closed-set knowledge selection on 3.2; continue to follow `references/3.2-conversion-transplant.md` for 3.2 unless the user explicitly asks to merge the modes.

The two controls are intentionally asymmetric:

- Codex is closed-set at the content-design stage.
- Gemini is not closed-set at the expansion stage. It receives the complete standard 2.5 prompt, the fixed mother-topic exact-lock block, and the frozen rebuilt source. It may add explanations, mechanisms, scenes, analogies, examples, deductions, and the conversion text already required by old 2.5. The raw response is immutable evidence and every returned content unit is retained in the accepted body.

Do not add a knowledge-closure instruction, safety layer, CTA-free override, or variable conversion prompt to Gemini. The fixed mother-topic exact-lock block is the only allowed 3.1 workflow addition to the complete old 2.5 block. Its article-specific values come only from the locked source-body evidence; it constrains the public topic and promised count without weakening the old 2.5 content or conversion instructions.

## Workflow

1. Read `references/mother-topic-exact-lock.md`. Extract and read the source body. Copy a verbatim source-body quote and short exact anchor for the public mother topic before retrieval. Infer the audience promise, relationship stage, emotional direction, and any promised count from the body, not from the filename or metadata. The anchor and public topic wording are immutable unless the user explicitly changes the mother topic.
2. Build or refresh a knowledge-call pack for that source. Retrieve same-topic B cards, page-anchored external atomic knowledge, Gary anonymized composite case candidates, cross-source modules, same-source 3.1/3.2 exclusions, recent cross-article saturation, and opening matches. Treat the call-pack title and `public_topic` as retrieval metadata only; they may not rename the source-body mother topic.
3. Exclude accepted same-source routes and recently saturated route signatures. Rejected history excludes only the failed method, not the whole knowledge family.
4. Select enough knowledge cards/modules to fulfill the source's promised count and causal spine. Prefer a combination whose points are mutually distinct and naturally belong under the mother topic. If the first set is insufficient, broaden retrieval across unused same-topic B/X cards, page-anchored external atomic blocks, cross-source modules, and eligible composite candidates; replace weak slots and polish the combination before declaring a missing slot. Never fill the gap from general model knowledge.
5. Bind every planned point to at least one exact knowledge source ID such as `B-070`, `X-013`, or an external atomic `*-K####` ID. A point may combine compatible modules, but it may not be supported only by Codex judgment. Prefer the page-anchored atomic block over a source-title card when it supplies the actual mechanism or operation.
6. Create a provenance map before writing the rebuilt source. For every point record: selected card/module, retained knowledge, perspective adaptation, excluded collision, and intended scene/transition.
7. Write the short rebuilt source. Codex may select, order, merge compatible non-duplicate knowledge, convert female-to-male perspective when semantically valid, polish wording, add transitions, and preserve the mother topic/count. Do not put a CTA, fan-group handoff, account self-introduction, or fixed ending in the frozen source. Codex may not invent an independent viewpoint, mechanism, case, operation, rule, or fifth point. When a case is needed, use a supplied Gary record or a retrieved `GCOMP-*` candidate. A retrieved candidate starts as `external_case_candidate_requires_adaptation`: open its card, use one continuous source scene, extract the four proof fields, and only then record the adopted run as `adapted_anonymized_composite`. The oral manuscript may use natural Gary first-person narration while the sourced result boundary remains preserved.
   - Default experience-proof rule: every core point receives one source-supported experience judgment inside the frozen source. This is one judgment function per point, not one repeated sentence template per point.
   - Vary the proof operation across the article. Suitable forms include a small-decision observation, display/profile audit, reaction-timing check, message-sequence comparison, continuous-behavior test, written screening-standard comparison, or another topic-native diagnostic supported by the selected knowledge. In a long same-topic list, one evidence lane may recur only when the observable criterion, scene, and diagnostic purpose remain materially different.
   - Write the judgment as natural source prose so Gemini can absorb it into the argument. Do not label it `经验锚点`, `学员案例`, `实战证明`, or expose the construction rule.
   - Do not fabricate client volume, names, screenshots, dates, guaranteed results, successful reversals, or unsupported Gary authority. Natural first-person judgment is allowed only when the actual diagnostic criteria and causal conclusion remain traceable to the selected knowledge.
   - Do not append an experience-density/count/type supplement to Gemini. Do not ask Gemini to place one experience block under every point. Do not inject or append experience paragraphs after Gemini generation. If an experience judgment is weak or repetitive, repair the frozen source and rerun the complete isolated request.
8. Apply the deletion test: after removing all wording traceable only to Codex, the remaining substantive points must still be fully supported by the cited knowledge cards/modules. Transitions and surface phrasing may disappear; the argument must not.
9. Keep the source's original force band. Do not turn hard, dark, interest-sensitive material into safe communication education, boundary management, neutral counseling, or relationship-governance language. Do not add visible defensive qualifiers such as `不是操控`, `不是控制`, or `不是拿捏` unless the mother topic itself requires that distinction.
10. Run `scripts/validate_mother_topic_lock.py` against the rebuilt source. Freeze only after the exact anchor survives and no derived umbrella label has replaced it. Save the provenance record before model expansion. Record `mother_topic_source_quote`, `mother_topic_anchor`, `mother_topic_public_wording`, `mother_topic_exact_lock_pass`, `knowledge_pack_title_ignored_for_topic: true`, `codex_content_invention: false`, and `gemini_closed_set_restriction: false`. Also record `experience_observations` with one entry per core point: point number, proof function, observable evidence, supporting knowledge IDs, and whether its expression duplicates another point. Freeze only when every core point has one traceable judgment and the article avoids repeating the same proof operation, observable criterion, scene, diagnostic purpose, and sentence shell as a package.
11. Use `scripts/build_31_gemini_prompt.py` to assemble the complete standard `2.5 Direct Draft` prompt, the fixed mother-topic exact-lock block below, and the frozen rebuilt source. Send that prompt through a fresh `--isolated` Gemini request. Do not send knowledge IDs, vault paths, history notes, provenance, exclusions, article-specific CTA wording, or another supplement to Gemini.
12. Save Gemini's response unchanged as the raw artifact. Do not run the legacy conversion cleaner or zero-CTA validator. Do not delete Doubao/AI-summary guidance, engagement prompts, fan-group/contact language, account introduction, examples, repeated CTA units, strong wording, or any other Gemini content. Retry Gemini only when the mother topic/count drifts, the length gate fails, or the output is a refusal/stale-topic response.
13. Validate the raw body for exact mother topic, promised count, Gary force band, length, and absence of stale-route or backstage contamination. Then read and apply `references/3.1-cta-layer.md`: preserve every Gemini content unit in order, add no Codex CTA, and replace only the terminal ending with `我是探花Gary，我们粉丝群里见，感谢观看。` Narrow non-deleting grammar/TTS repairs must be recorded as exact before/after pairs. Before Word export, verify complete point-level provenance in the frozen source.
14. Save the Gemini-retention manifest. Export the normal title package, four-opening package, and正文 Word. Yellow-highlight only the normalized fixed ending by default and no other text. Do not run LibreOffice, `soffice`, PDF conversion, PNG rendering, `render_docx.py`, or any visual-render retry for 3.1. Run the unified final validator once after saving the Word:


The manifest must use mode `3.1_gemini_full_retention`, record the immutable raw path/hash, `gemini_content_preserved: true`, `removed_content: []`, exact mechanical repairs, `terminal_ending_before`, exact `fixed_ending`, and `highlight_texts: [fixed_ending]`. The single report must pass the mother-topic lock, minimum length, exact ending, required four-opening headings, raw-to-final retention equality, Word-body equality, DOCX ZIP integrity, mother-topic presence, and exact yellow-highlight equality. Use `--allow-no-yellow` only when the user explicitly requests an unhighlighted clean copy. ## Codex Closed-Set Boundary

Allowed Codex work:

- semantic retrieval and ranking;
- same-source and recent-route exclusion;
- combining two compatible knowledge modules into one point without adding a new causal claim;
- converting viewpoint, pronouns, and examples only when the source meaning remains intact;
- shortening, smoothing, sequencing, and writing natural transitions;
- adapting the knowledge to the source's public count and relationship stage;
- writing a topic-native fan-group diagnostic bridge from knowledge already selected.
- polishing and transplanting a retrieved external atomic block while retaining its source and page anchor;
- adapting a retrieved `GCOMP-*` candidate from one continuous source scene into natural Gary narration without stitching dispersed automatic clues or inventing names, dates, screenshots, exact client volume, or an outcome absent from the source.

Forbidden Codex work:

- inventing a stronger fifth mechanism because the retrieved set feels incomplete;
- filling a missing promised item with general model knowledge;
- adding a case, operation, script, theory, or causal link that no selected card/module supports;
- changing the mother topic to fit the available knowledge;
- adopting a knowledge-pack title, B/X card title, history title, or Codex summary as the public mother topic;
- replacing a source label such as `四样东西 / 致命需求` with `心理待遇`, `心理营养`, or another unified abstraction;
- using the old source's rejected route as an uncited filler;
- presenting Gemini's later additions as if they came from the knowledge base.
- copying the same Gary-consultation sentence across all points, changing only the topic noun;
- claiming `我见过很多/我带过很多/群里大量案例都证明` without traceable support;
- adding experience judgments after Gemini generation to satisfy a density target.

If the knowledge base still cannot support the full promised count or causal spine after the required broader retrieval and traceable-module replacement pass, stop the freeze step and report the exact missing slot. Offer either to add/import knowledge first or to use the explicit legacy Codex-designed 3.1 mode. Never silently switch modes.

## Gemini Freedom Boundary

Gemini remains governed by the full standard 2.5 content/style prompt plus the fixed mother-topic exact-lock block. It may add:

- deeper psychology explanations;
- topic-consistent domain terms;
- new scenes, analogies, examples, dialogue, and deductions;
- stronger old-2.5 language and consequences;
- connective material needed to reach long-form depth.

Gemini may not change the frozen mother topic, omit or add promised list items, replace the frozen route with a generic Gary route, import a prior article's topic, or expose the production process. Its returned conversion units, examples, account language, and strong wording are all accepted content and remain in the final body. Codex adds no conversion layer; it normalizes only the terminal fixed ending.

Do not automatically write Gemini-added mechanisms back into B cards or cross-source modules. They enter only the D-layer generated-draft record. Promotion into reusable knowledge requires later user confirmation and, where applicable, data review.

## Acceptance Gate

Reject or stop when:

- any frozen-source point lacks an exact B/X source mapping;
- an external claim is used from a source-title card while the exact supporting atomic block and page anchor are absent;
- a `GCOMP-*` case is presented internally as a verified Gary student record, or gains a name, date, screenshot, exact result, or success ending not found in its source;
- Codex invented a substantive point, mechanism, case, operation, or causal bridge;
- any core point lacks one traceable experience judgment in the frozen source;
- multiple experience judgments repeat the same proof operation, observable criterion, diagnostic purpose, and Gary identity/consultation sentence with only nouns replaced;
- the Gemini prompt contains a separate experience-density/count/type instruction, or experience paragraphs are appended after Gemini generation;
- selected knowledge does not naturally fit the mother topic or relationship stage;
- the exact source-derived mother-topic anchor is absent from the frozen or final body;
- a knowledge-pack title, card summary, or new umbrella label replaces or organizes the public mother topic;
- the promised count cannot be fully supported by distinct knowledge;
- the new route collides with accepted same-source history;
- the route becomes safer, milder, or more neutral than the source merely to avoid collision;
- the frozen source contains knowledge IDs, vault paths, prompt notes, or production language;
- Gemini is given a second-layer closure, free-form article supplement, CTA-free override, or variable CTA instruction beyond the fixed mother-topic exact-lock block;
- Gemini changes topic/count, reuses a stale route, returns fewer than 4000 Chinese characters, or promotes an internal mechanism/summary label into the public mother topic;
- the raw Gemini response is overwritten, any Gemini content unit is deleted or reordered, `removed_content` is nonempty, or the final body differs from the raw body beyond recorded mechanical repairs and terminal-ending replacement;
- post-processing rewrites Gemini's intensity or substantive content, inserts a Codex CTA, or performs an unrecorded wording change;
- the final 3.1 Word fails to highlight only the normalized fixed ending, highlights any Gemini content, or lacks the retention manifest;
- the 3.1 Word omits either the verbatim source opening or the separate source-opening optimized version, version three is no longer verbatim, version four changes the mother topic/count/route, or version four was inserted into the accepted body;
- LibreOffice, `soffice`, PDF/PNG rendering, `render_docx.py`, or a visual-render retry is invoked for a 3.1 text-first Word deliverable;
- the unified final validator fails, is skipped, or is followed by redundant standalone mother-topic, forbidden-term, ending, or Word-package checks;
- the generated draft is written back as confirmed knowledge before the user confirms it.
