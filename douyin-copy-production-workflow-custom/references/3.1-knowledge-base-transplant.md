Read this file in full whenever the user asks for `3.1`, `3.1换芯`, `3.1扩写`, `2.5预换芯`, `原稿预换芯`, or asks to reuse the approved knowledge-base-assisted test.

## Canonical Meaning

The default 3.1 workflow is now:

`body-derived mother topic -> knowledge-base retrieval and history exclusion -> Codex selection/polish/transplant without content invention -> freeze short source -> complete standard 2.5 prompt plus fixed CTA-free override -> isolated Gemini content expansion -> Codex balanced soft placements plus mid CTA and fixed ending -> exact yellow-highlight Word`

This replaces the former default in which Codex independently invented the replacement viewpoints. The former mode remains available only when the user explicitly asks for `旧版3.1`, `让Codex设计新观点`, or another unambiguous equivalent.

This update changes 3.1 only. Do not silently impose Codex closed-set knowledge selection on 3.2; continue to follow `references/3.2-conversion-transplant.md` for 3.2 unless the user explicitly asks to merge the modes.

The two controls are intentionally asymmetric:

- Codex is closed-set at the content-design stage.
- Gemini is not closed-set at the expansion stage. It receives the complete standard 2.5 prompt, the fixed CTA-free 3.1 override, and the frozen rebuilt source. It may add explanations, mechanisms, scenes, analogies, examples, and deductions inside the frozen mother topic and promised-point skeleton, but it must not generate conversion text or the fixed ending.

Do not add a knowledge-closure instruction, article-specific supplement, safety layer, or variable conversion prompt to Gemini. The fixed CTA-free 3.1 override is the only allowed workflow-level addition to the complete old 2.5 block; it prevents Gemini from writing CTA while leaving normal 2.5 content expansion intact.

## Workflow

1. Read `references/mother-topic-exact-lock.md`. Extract and read the source body. Copy a verbatim source-body quote and short exact anchor for the public mother topic before retrieval. Infer the audience promise, relationship stage, emotional direction, and any promised count from the body, not from the filename or metadata. The anchor and public topic wording are immutable unless the user explicitly changes the mother topic.
2. Build or refresh a knowledge-call pack for that source. Retrieve same-topic B cards, page-anchored external atomic knowledge, Gary anonymized composite case candidates, cross-source modules, same-source 3.1/3.2 exclusions, recent cross-article saturation, and opening matches. Treat the call-pack title and `public_topic` as retrieval metadata only; they may not rename the source-body mother topic.
3. Exclude accepted same-source routes and recently saturated route signatures. Rejected history excludes only the failed method, not the whole knowledge family.
4. Select enough knowledge cards/modules to fulfill the source's promised count and causal spine. Prefer a combination whose points are mutually distinct and naturally belong under the mother topic.
5. Bind every planned point to at least one exact knowledge source ID such as `B-070`, `X-013`, or an external atomic `*-K####` ID. A point may combine compatible modules, but it may not be supported only by Codex judgment. Prefer the page-anchored atomic block over a source-title card when it supplies the actual mechanism or operation.
6. Create a provenance map before writing the rebuilt source. For every point record: selected card/module, retained knowledge, perspective adaptation, excluded collision, and intended scene/transition.
7. Write the short rebuilt source. Codex may select, order, merge compatible non-duplicate knowledge, convert female-to-male perspective when semantically valid, polish wording, add transitions, and preserve the mother topic/count. Do not put a CTA, fan-group handoff, account self-introduction, or fixed ending in the frozen source. Codex may not invent an independent viewpoint, mechanism, case, operation, rule, or fifth point. When a case is needed, use a supplied Gary record or a retrieved `GCOMP-*` candidate. A retrieved candidate starts as `external_case_candidate_requires_adaptation`: open its card, use one continuous source scene, extract the four proof fields, and only then record the adopted run as `adapted_anonymized_composite`. The oral manuscript may use natural Gary first-person narration while the sourced result boundary remains preserved.
8. Apply the deletion test: after removing all wording traceable only to Codex, the remaining substantive points must still be fully supported by the cited knowledge cards/modules. Transitions and surface phrasing may disappear; the argument must not.
9. Keep the source's original force band. Do not turn hard, dark, interest-sensitive material into safe communication education, boundary management, neutral counseling, or relationship-governance language. Do not add visible defensive qualifiers such as `不是操控`, `不是控制`, or `不是拿捏` unless the mother topic itself requires that distinction.
10. Run `scripts/validate_mother_topic_lock.py` against the rebuilt source. Freeze only after the exact anchor survives and no derived umbrella label has replaced it. Save the provenance record before model expansion. Record `mother_topic_source_quote`, `mother_topic_anchor`, `mother_topic_public_wording`, `mother_topic_exact_lock_pass`, `knowledge_pack_title_ignored_for_topic: true`, `codex_content_invention: false`, and `gemini_closed_set_restriction: false`.
11. Send the complete standard `2.5 Direct Draft` prompt, then the fixed CTA-free 3.1 override below, then the frozen rebuilt source through a fresh `--isolated` Gemini request. Do not send knowledge IDs, vault paths, history notes, provenance, exclusions, article-specific CTA wording, or another supplement to Gemini.
12. Run `python3 scripts/validate_31_gemini_no_cta.py --input <gemini-raw> --report-out <cta-free-report.json>`. Reject and retry if it reports any CTA, fan-group invitation, account self-introduction, or fixed ending. Do not accept and delete generated CTA afterward. Treat the CTA-free returned language as the default accepted content surface. Perform only narrow mechanical cleanup for unmistakable typos, prompt leakage, or a hard operational-boundary defect; do not calm or professionalize it. Then read and apply `references/3.1-balanced-soft-fan-group-layer.md`: Codex adds the article-native soft placements, one topic-native mid CTA by default, and the exact fixed ending in a single post-generation pass.
13. Before Word export, perform the semantic checks that cannot be reduced to string matching: topic/count delivery, Gary force band, absence of old-route contamination, and complete point-level knowledge provenance in the frozen source. Do not run the final mother-topic, forbidden-term, ending, or Word checks separately at this stage; they are combined after export.
14. Save a conversion insertion manifest and export the normal title package, four-opening package, and正文 Word. Yellow-highlight every manifest entry by default and no other text. Do not run LibreOffice, `soffice`, PDF conversion, PNG rendering, `render_docx.py`, or any visual-render retry for 3.1. Run the unified final validator once after saving the Word:

```bash
python3 scripts/validate_31_final_package.py \
  --source <source> \
  --frozen <frozen-source> \
  --final <accepted-body> \
  --docx <final-word> \
  --anchor '<exact-source-body-anchor>' \
  --insertion-manifest <conversion-insertions.json> \
  --forbidden-label '<known-rejected-umbrella-label>' \
  --report-out <unified-final-qa.json>
```

The manifest must contain exact `soft_placements`, `mid_cta`, `fixed_ending`, and ordered `highlight_texts`. The single report must pass the final mother-topic lock, minimum length, built-in and task-specific forbidden terms, exact fixed ending, required four-opening headings, Word-body equality, DOCX ZIP integrity, mother-topic presence in Word, and exact equality between Word's yellow runs and `highlight_texts`. Add task-specific terms with repeated `--forbidden-term` and rejected umbrella labels with repeated `--forbidden-label`. Use `--allow-no-yellow` only when the user explicitly requests an unhighlighted clean copy. After the report passes, do not rerun those checks individually. Register the route, CTA-free Gemini result, soft placements, Codex mid CTA, insertion manifest, and unified report path in the 3.1 ledger. Then register the draft under one stable work ID with status `generated` until the user confirms it.

## 3.1 Four-Opening Package

Every 3.1 Word export must contain these four standalone candidates before the body:

1. `开头版本一：高阶认知课式开头`
2. `开头版本二：身份点名式硬核学习开头`
3. `开头版本三：保留原文开头（来自源文档）`
4. `开头版本四：原文开头优化版（贴合正文）`

Version three is evidence preservation. Extract it from the source body, keep the source order and wording verbatim, and never replace it with the optimized version.

Version four is a separate source-derived usable opening. Build it only after the 3.1 body has been accepted, using both the verbatim source opening and the accepted body:

- preserve the exact public mother-topic anchor, original attack direction, signature claim, and force band;
- repair broken syntax, punctuation, repetition, and line fragmentation so it reads naturally aloud;
- retain the source opening's distinctive social, emotional, or dark judgment instead of converting it into a generic course template;
- align its mechanism names, promised count, point order, and final bridge with the accepted body's actual route;
- end with a natural structure promise or transition that can connect directly to the accepted body's first substantive paragraph;
- do not add a new mother topic, independent viewpoint, unsupported mechanism, case, CTA, Doubao instruction, or safety disclaimer;
- do not insert version four into, prepend it to, or otherwise rewrite the accepted body. It remains a standalone candidate before `正文`.

If the source opening is very short, version four may complete its syntax and add one body-supported bridge. It may not manufacture a new hook unrelated to the source. Save both opening texts in the run package and verify before export that version three still matches the source exactly while version four matches the final body's actual count and route.

## Codex Closed-Set Boundary

Allowed Codex work:

- semantic retrieval and ranking;
- same-source and recent-route exclusion;
- combining two compatible knowledge modules into one point without adding a new causal claim;
- converting viewpoint, pronouns, and examples only when the source meaning remains intact;
- shortening, smoothing, sequencing, and writing natural transitions;
- adapting the knowledge to the source's public count and relationship stage;
- writing a topic-native fan-group diagnostic bridge from knowledge already selected.
- polishing and transplanting a retrieved external atomic block while retaining its source and page anchor;
- adapting a retrieved `GCOMP-*` candidate from one continuous source scene into natural Gary narration without stitching dispersed automatic clues or inventing names, dates, screenshots, exact client volume, or an outcome absent from the source.

Forbidden Codex work:

- inventing a stronger fifth mechanism because the retrieved set feels incomplete;
- filling a missing promised item with general model knowledge;
- adding a case, operation, script, theory, or causal link that no selected card/module supports;
- changing the mother topic to fit the available knowledge;
- adopting a knowledge-pack title, B/X card title, history title, or Codex summary as the public mother topic;
- replacing a source label such as `四样东西 / 致命需求` with `心理待遇`, `心理营养`, or another unified abstraction;
- using the old source's rejected route as an uncited filler;
- presenting Gemini's later additions as if they came from the knowledge base.

If the knowledge base cannot support the full promised count or causal spine, stop the freeze step and report the exact missing slot. Offer either to add/import knowledge first or to use the explicit legacy Codex-designed 3.1 mode. Never silently switch modes.

## Gemini Freedom Boundary

Gemini remains governed by the full standard 2.5 content/style prompt plus the fixed CTA-free override. It may add:

- deeper psychology explanations;
- topic-consistent domain terms;
- new scenes, analogies, examples, dialogue, and deductions;
- stronger old-2.5 language and consequences;
- connective material needed to reach long-form depth.

Gemini may not change the frozen mother topic, omit or add promised list items, replace the frozen route with a generic Gary route, import a prior article's topic, expose the production process, or generate any CTA/fan-group/account-ending content. These are normal 3.1 topic/quality gates, not a closed-set knowledge restriction. Codex adds the fixed ending only after the CTA-free body is accepted.

Do not automatically write Gemini-added mechanisms back into B cards or cross-source modules. They enter only the D-layer generated-draft record. Promotion into reusable knowledge requires later user confirmation and, where applicable, data review.

## Acceptance Gate

Reject or stop when:

- any frozen-source point lacks an exact B/X source mapping;
- an external claim is used from a source-title card while the exact supporting atomic block and page anchor are absent;
- a `GCOMP-*` case is presented internally as a verified Gary student record, or gains a name, date, screenshot, exact result, or success ending not found in its source;
- Codex invented a substantive point, mechanism, case, operation, or causal bridge;
- selected knowledge does not naturally fit the mother topic or relationship stage;
- the exact source-derived mother-topic anchor is absent from the frozen or final body;
- a knowledge-pack title, card summary, or new umbrella label replaces or organizes the public mother topic;
- the promised count cannot be fully supported by distinct knowledge;
- the new route collides with accepted same-source history;
- the route becomes safer, milder, or more neutral than the source merely to avoid collision;
- the frozen source contains knowledge IDs, vault paths, prompt notes, or production language;
- Gemini is given a second-layer closure, article supplement, or variable CTA instruction beyond the fixed CTA-free override;
- Gemini changes topic/count, reuses a stale route, returns fewer than 4000 Chinese characters, or generates any CTA, fan-group invitation, account self-introduction, or fixed ending;
- post-processing rewrites Gemini's intensity rather than performing narrow mechanical cleanup;
- the Codex conversion layer omits the default mid CTA, writes a generic or oversized CTA, repeats a standalone `粉丝群里……` paragraph pattern, or changes substantive article content;
- the final 3.1 Word fails to highlight every Codex-added soft placement, mid CTA, and fixed ending, highlights any non-manifest text, or lacks the insertion manifest;
- the 3.1 Word omits either the verbatim source opening or the separate source-opening optimized version, version three is no longer verbatim, version four changes the mother topic/count/route, or version four was inserted into the accepted body;
- LibreOffice, `soffice`, PDF/PNG rendering, `render_docx.py`, or a visual-render retry is invoked for a 3.1 text-first Word deliverable;
- the unified final validator fails, is skipped, or is followed by redundant standalone mother-topic, forbidden-term, ending, or Word-package checks;
- the generated draft is written back as confirmed knowledge before the user confirms it.
