# Public Mother-Topic Exact Lock

Read this file for every male Gary rewrite, expansion, pre-transplant, finished-draft transplant, 3.1, 3.5, 3.2, fixed-topic cycle, or knowledge-base-assisted rewrite.

## Non-Negotiable Rule

The public mother topic must remain the source body's mother topic. Only content underneath that mother topic may be replaced.

The mother topic is the source body's public question, result promise, central object, and attack direction. It is not the filename, folder name, document title, knowledge-pack title, Obsidian card title, model-generated summary, planning label, or a later packaging title.

Source-body evidence wins over every derived artifact unless the user explicitly supplies a different mother topic for the current manuscript.

For example:

- `女人最想要男人的四样东西 / 四个致命需求` must not become `四种心理待遇`, `四种心理营养`, `四种关系需求`, or another umbrella label;
- `从认识推进到暧昧` must not become `关系权限逐级开放` or `关系阶段管理`;
- `让女人主动付出` must not become `建立双向参与`;
- `十二个顶级黑暗思维` must remain that mother topic even when all twelve internal rules are replaced.

An internal planning label may exist in a private route record, but it may not replace, precede, summarize, rename, or become the public organizing label of the source mother topic.

## Required Lock Record

Before selecting knowledge or designing replacement content, record:

```json
{
  "mother_topic_source_quote": "<verbatim body quote that states the mother topic>",
  "mother_topic_anchor": "<short exact public wording that must survive>",
  "mother_topic_public_wording": "<the wording used in the frozen source and final body>",
  "mother_topic_exact_lock_pass": true,
  "knowledge_pack_title_ignored_for_topic": true,
  "alternate_umbrella_labels": []
}
```

The `mother_topic_anchor` must be copied from the source body, not rewritten by Codex. Keep the source's central nouns, target result, conflict object, and attack direction. Grammatical particles may be removed only when the anchor still appears as the same contiguous public wording.

The source's explicit count remains fixed by default. Change it only when the user explicitly authorizes a count change; a changed count still cannot change the mother topic.

## What May Change Under The Locked Topic

Subject to the selected mode's narrower rules, the following may be replaced beneath the locked mother topic:

- list items or supporting viewpoints;
- concrete operations, scripts, micro-steps, and timing;
- examples, cases, dialogue, scenes, metaphors, and proof chains;
- supporting mechanisms and professional terms;
- paragraph order, transitions, objection handling, and conversion evidence.

Every replacement must still answer the original public mother topic. If knowledge retrieval cannot supply enough distinct content under that exact topic, stop and report the missing slot. Never rename the topic to fit available knowledge.

## Knowledge-Base Boundary

Knowledge cards and call packs supply material under the mother topic. They never name or redefine the mother topic.

Ignore these fields when locking the public topic:

- knowledge-call-pack `title` or `public_topic` generated by a retrieval script;
- B/X card title, mother-topic fingerprint, or summary label;
- history-card title or prior output title;
- filename, Word title, folder name, or metadata.

Use those fields only for retrieval and traceability. The source body remains the authority.

## Two Mandatory Gates

### Before Freeze

Reject the rebuilt source when:

- the exact source-derived anchor is absent;
- a new umbrella label replaces the source wording;
- the first public promise, numbered-list introduction, summary paragraph, or CTA describes a renamed mother topic;
- deleting the new label restores the original mother topic's clarity or aggression;
- the provenance record claims `codex_content_invention: false` while the public mother-topic label was invented or borrowed from a knowledge artifact.

### After Expansion

Reject and rerun when:

- Gemini omits or renames the locked anchor;
- the hook, list introduction, summary, CTA, Word title, or filename promotes a different umbrella topic;
- the original mother topic survives only as an incidental sentence while another label organizes the article.

Do not repair a mother-topic drift only in the filename or title package. Rebuild the frozen source first, then rerun the full mode from that corrected source.

## Deterministic Check

Run:

```bash
python3 scripts/validate_mother_topic_lock.py \
  --source <source-file> \
  --frozen <frozen-source.txt> \
  --final <accepted-body.txt> \
  --anchor '<exact source-body anchor>' \
  --forbidden-label '<rejected replacement label>'
```

The deterministic check verifies exact anchor survival and explicitly known replacement labels. It supplements semantic review; it does not authorize a new synonym or abstraction merely because that label was not listed.
