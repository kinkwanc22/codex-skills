# 2.5 Finished-Draft Transplant / 2.5成稿换芯

Use this mode only when the user supplies an already-expanded, user-approved old 2.5 manuscript and asks to keep its feel while replacing the internal content. It is distinct from routine `2.5 Direct Draft`.

## Current Canonical Mode

As of 2026-08-08, `2.5成稿换芯` keeps the fixed-mother-topic cycle with limited inheritance, but its base 2.5 voice now uses the tested 2026-06-05 early B direct-draft prompt:

`complete 2026-06-05 early B 2.5 block -> strong-attack transplant supplement -> article-specific unused route -> limited-inheritance card -> full approved old-2.5 finished draft`

This mode supersedes both the earlier layer-only transplant prompt and the earlier full-exclusion transplant mode. Never call the strong-attack transplant persona or task layer without first loading the complete 2026-06-05 early B 2.5 block. The early B block controls Ryan voice, opening formula, spoken rhythm, case texture, mechanism density, and continuous deduction; the supplement controls mother-topic preservation, new-body construction, limited inheritance, exclusion of copied old material, and attack-level retention.

## Fixed Outcome

Preserve:

- the mother topic and its audience promise;
- the approved old 2.5 pressure, Ryan voice, rough spoken rhythm, rhetorical questions, analogies, uneven pacing, and abrupt hard conclusions;
- the source's central causal mechanism or distinctive conceptual bloodline;
- one or two short signature sentences when they still fit naturally, each used at most once.

Replace:

- the old list items or internal argument chain;
- signature examples, dialogue, set pieces, and proof scenes;
- old metaphors or phrases that would make the new draft feel like a paraphrase.

The correct result is `same mother topic + limited source bloodline + same expressive DNA + genuinely new body`, not a renamed old draft, not a continuation that refers to an earlier episode, and not a safe rewrite. As a working ratio, inherited source content should occupy about 20%-30%; the new route should occupy about 70%-80%.

## Required Preparation

1. Extract the body of the supplied `.docx`, normally beginning after the `正文` heading.
2. Build three separate notes:
   - `style genes`: opening pressure, address terms, sentence rhythm, analogy type, list pacing, transition style, CTA placement, and ending cadence;
   - `inherited bloodline`: one central source mechanism plus zero to two short signature sentences worth retaining;
   - `excluded content genes`: old list items, signature cases, dialogue, metaphors, scene order, proof order, and conclusions that must not return.
3. For every retained mechanism, require at least two new realities: a new scene, interest shift, relationship consequence, or contrary proof that the source did not use.
4. Design an unused internal route before calling Gemini. Use ordinary spoken judgments, not abstract course-module labels.
5. Keep the mother topic fixed. A promised number may change only when the user permits it or the mother topic has no fixed-number promise. Every promised item must be delivered.
6. Check the cumulative route ledger before generation. Do not reuse another article's mechanism chain, list architecture, scene sequence, or central metaphor.

## Validation Gate

Reject and retry when any condition fails:

- fewer than 4000 Chinese characters;
- mother topic changed or diluted;
- promised list count is incomplete;
- an old item was merely renamed or reordered;
- an old signature case, dialogue, or metaphor returns;
- the selected source mechanism disappears completely, or retained source material grows beyond the limited-inheritance role;
- a retained mechanism lacks at least two new scenes, interest shifts, consequences, or contrary proofs;
- the draft contains serial/backstage wording and cannot stand alone;
- the draft becomes safe psychology education or foregrounds defensive qualifiers;
- the draft exposes structure labels other than the intended spoken list heads;
- no mid-article CTA, no ending CTA, or the fixed final sentence is not exact;
- `内部群`, `私董会`, risk notes, highlights, confirmation language, or backend traces appear.

Before Word export, run a wording pass for invented or awkward expressions. Fix only obvious local defects, such as `性缩力` -> `吸引力会直接崩塌`; do not use local polishing to soften the article or replace its argument.

For batch work, also compare each accepted body against earlier accepted bodies and route fingerprints. Treat a high similarity result as a review trigger rather than proof by itself; inspect whether the same mechanism order, scene chain, paragraph movement, or conclusion pattern has returned.

## Batch Rhythm And Records

For more than three manuscripts, produce in groups of three. Each article still runs through `--session-file work/gemini_session_female_25_transplant_legacy.json`; a group is a quality-control cadence, not a shared prompt, and this female conversation is allowed to accumulate only this transplant mode.

For every group:

1. prepare three independent content cards and prompts;
2. generate and validate each article separately;
3. retry a failed article from the full prompt rather than locally padding a short or off-topic draft;
4. accept the group only after all three pass;
5. append the accepted route, mechanism fingerprint, character count, retry history, and output path to the cumulative ledgers before starting the next group.

Keep these batch records in the delivery folder:

- `女性版已用内容路线台账.json`;
- `女性版失败重试记录.json`;
- a batch/final validation report;
- working prompts and accepted bodies under `_work/` when the user wants a reproducible production archive.

Do not start thirty model requests at once. The approved cadence is three at a time because it reduces style drift and makes route duplication visible before it spreads through the batch.
